# presidential claims and supporting evidence

[all research](../INDEX.md) · [how to read evidence](../READING_GUIDE.md)

**research status: under verification**

## what this is about

test falsifiable presidential claims against the relevant evidence, separating repetition from accuracy.

## why it is examined

make the claim-family denominator, scoring rules and independent review inspectable.

## what is documented

the frozen high-salience layer contains 78 scoreable claim families; a separate 50-claim control and blind second pass are preserved.

## what remains uncertain

the repeat-search and persistence audit are incomplete. the selected high-salience layer cannot estimate the accuracy of all presidential speech. factual discrepancy does not establish intent.

## research status

under verification. this label describes the research work, not guilt, legal liability or the truth of every claim. source dates and evidence grades remain those in the linked records; this navigation audit adds no new findings.
