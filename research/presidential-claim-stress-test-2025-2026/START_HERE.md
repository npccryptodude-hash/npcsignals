## what is this study asking?

this study compares selected public claims by donald trump with evidence about the same subject and time period. related statements are grouped into a “claim family” so repetition does not count as several independent claims.

the selected sample cannot tell us how accurate all presidential speech is. a factual discrepancy does not establish intent.

## what does the deposited workbook show?

the [main evidence workbook](NPCsignals_Presidential_Claim_Stress_Test_Ledger_v1.0.xlsx) contains 79 claim-family rows. one is excluded from the accuracy calculation, leaving 78. the recorded classifications are:

- 9 supported;
- 9 mixed or misleading;
- 9 unsupported;
- 51 false or severe discrepancies.

these counts reproduce the workbook's classifications. reproducing the arithmetic does not independently verify each classification.

## an important verification limitation

the workbook contains conflicting descriptions of its second review. `Inter-Rater Analysis` contains 78 comparisons and records 64 agreements on accuracy. however, `Blind Second Pass` has no completed second-pass scores, and `Publication Audit` still says that no independent scorer has run it. the [separate blind workbook](<NPCsignals_Trump_Claim_Stress_Test_Blind_Second_Pass (1).xlsx>) is also a template with no completed scores.

comparison results are therefore present, but the deposited material does not resolve how they relate to the unfilled blind-review sheets and the pending status note. this guide does not present the independent-review process as verified.

the overview also reports a 50-claim control sample. a corresponding item-level control ledger was not located in this study's deposited files. its reported comparison with the main sample cannot be reproduced from these workbooks alone.

## how to inspect a claim without searching technical folders

1. open the main workbook and choose `Public Ledger v1.0`.
2. find the claim identifier in column a; column e gives the claim text.
3. column k links to the original statement where retained; column m links to the comparison evidence. 18 of the 79 rows have no statement url in column k. some comparison sources are journalism rather than primary records.
4. read the validation status and limitations before using the row as evidence. the current accuracy classification is in column w.

for example, the statement source for `L001` is the [4 march 2025 congressional address transcript, preserved by the american presidency project](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/address-before-joint-session-the-congress-4). this establishes what was said; it does not by itself establish the claim's accuracy.
