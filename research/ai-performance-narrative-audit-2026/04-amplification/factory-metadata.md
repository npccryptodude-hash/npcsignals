# FACTORY metadata case

## Purpose

FACTORY is used to examine how an amplified AI-performance narrative became part of the public provenance of a tradable asset.

## Direct captured market pages

Two GeckoTerminal captures obtained during the audit show the FACTORY market page at materially different stages of the asset's price history.

The captures establish:

- the existence of the FACTORY market
- the token label `Grok Token Factory`
- the visible creator-wallet prefix `HAh7q...khpV`
- a description referencing `@enkoxbt`
- Pump.fun / PumpSwap context

The screenshots are useful market and metadata captures, but GeckoTerminal is a third-party market interface rather than the primary Pump.fun metadata source.

## Additional provenance material

During the audit, FACTORY-related metadata and discovery records were observed referencing:

- `@enkoxbt`
- Enko-linked GitHub material
- Enko public content
- RoundtableSpace status `2093824765785976942`

Language describing fees as redirected to a GitHub identity was also observed in downstream material.

The exact primary Pump.fun token page and mint remain a source-hardening target.

## Finding FACTORY-01

**Current verdict:** SUPPORTED pending primary metadata hardening  
**Current evidence grade:** E2 on the source package currently preserved  
**Scope:** token metadata / provenance relationship only

The available captures and downstream metadata support that FACTORY publicly incorporated references to pre-existing AI/Grok creator material.

A stronger `CONFIRMED / E3` classification should require preservation of the primary Pump.fun metadata or equivalent primary technical evidence for the exact asset.

This is intentionally more conservative than treating a third-party market page as primary metadata.

## Not established

This does not establish that:

- Enko created the token
- Enko approved the token before launch
- RoundtableSpace created or approved the token
- either party controlled the launch wallet
- either party received creator-fee payouts
- the token launch was coordinated with the referenced creators

## Analytical significance

The key question is not whether amplification verified the upstream AI-performance narrative.

The relevant observation is that creator and amplifier material can become part of the public provenance of a financialized asset.

The evidentiary strength of that proposition must still track the quality of the metadata source.
