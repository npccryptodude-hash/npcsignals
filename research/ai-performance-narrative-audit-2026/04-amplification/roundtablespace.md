# RoundtableSpace amplification case

## Purpose

This case examines amplification as a distribution event, not as independent validation of an underlying performance claim.

## Direct captured post

A direct X capture under the display name `0xMarioNawfal` and handle `@RoundtableSpace` states:

> THIS GUY TAUGHT GROK BOT TO AUTONOMOUSLY LAUNCH MEMECOINS ON PUMPFUN, KILL FAILED LAUNCHES AND COLLECT CREATOR FEES FROM THE ONES THAT TRADE.

The post embeds Zynex-related video material showing a `GROK TOKEN FACTORY` interface.

Relevant X status recorded during the audit:

`2093824765785976942`

Source status for the captured post: `PRIMARY-CAPTURED`.

## Finding AMP-01

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED  
**Evidence grade:** E2  
**Scope:** public amplification and downstream provenance only

The captured post establishes that RoundtableSpace amplified the Grok-agent token-factory narrative.

Separate downstream FACTORY material examined during the audit referenced the same RoundtableSpace status, supporting the narrower conclusion that amplification later became part of token provenance.

## What amplification does not establish

The post does not independently verify:

- that the underlying bot performed as described
- that any displayed profit or fee total was historically realized
- that an autonomous system executed every displayed action
- that RoundtableSpace performed transaction-level verification before amplification

## Not established

The audit did not establish that RoundtableSpace:

- created FACTORY or another downstream token
- approved a downstream token
- controlled the identified launch wallet
- received creator fees
- financially benefited from the token
- knowingly repeated a false claim
- participated in a coordinated operation with the original creator or token launcher

## Analytical significance

The evidence supports a distribution event and a later provenance relationship.

It does not support transferring confidence from a large amplifier to the underlying performance claim.

This is a direct example of the rule:

**Distribution depth ≠ evidence depth.**
