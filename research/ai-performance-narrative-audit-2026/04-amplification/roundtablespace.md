# RoundtableSpace amplification case

## Purpose

This case examines amplification as a distribution event, not as independent validation of an underlying performance claim.

## Observed post

A captured RoundtableSpace post under the display name `0xMarioNawfal` and handle `@RoundtableSpace` stated:

`THIS GUY TAUGHT GROK BOT TO AUTONOMOUSLY LAUNCH MEMECOINS ON PUMPFUN, KILL FAILED LAUNCHES AND COLLECT CREATOR FEES FROM THE ONES THAT TRADE.`

The post amplified Zynex-related video material.

A relevant X status identified during the audit is:

`2093824765785976942`

Final repository publication should retain the original capture and URL as raw evidence.

## Finding AMP-01

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED  
**Evidence grade:** E2 where the captured post is combined with independent downstream metadata referencing the same status  
**Scope:** amplification and downstream provenance only

The post materially expanded distribution of the narrative, and the RoundtableSpace status later appeared in the provenance trail of a tokenized narrative case.

## Not established

The audit did not establish that RoundtableSpace:

- independently verified the underlying trade or bot performance
- created the later token
- approved the token
- controlled the token launch wallet
- received creator fees
- financially benefited from the token
- participated in a coordinated operation with the original creator or token launcher

## Analytical significance

Amplification increased reach.

It did not, by itself, increase transaction-level evidence for the underlying performance claim.

This is a direct example of the distinction between distribution depth and evidence depth.
