# Website entry — Distribution Depth Is Not Evidence Depth

## Title

**Distribution Depth Is Not Evidence Depth**

## Short intro

A stress test of viral AI-performance claims, amplification, tokenization, creator-fee infrastructure and post-launch creator onboarding.

## Core finding

**Financialization can move faster than verification.**

A real market move, repeated narrative, creator adoption or fee mechanism does not retroactively reconstruct the original performance claim.

## What the audit found

- Asset movement can be real while actor-level performance remains unverified.
- Repeated first-person narratives are not automatically independent evidence.
- Amplification can become part of token provenance without adding independent verification.
- Creator-fee capability is not the same as a verified payout.
- Post-launch creator onboarding is observable in more than one directly adjudicated case.
- Short timing intervals do not establish automation, coordination, prior knowledge or causality.
- One coordinated operation was **not established**.

## Evidence status

**Evidence Ledger v1.0 — frozen 2026-09-06.**

## Editorial guardrails

The website entry must not imply that:

- every AI-performance claim examined was false
- every referenced creator participated before token launch
- creator-fee routing statements prove completed payouts
- repeated narratives prove common account control
- short launch intervals prove automation or coordination
- all observed accounts or projects form one coordinated operation

The website summary should remain subordinate to the frozen Evidence Ledger v1.0.
