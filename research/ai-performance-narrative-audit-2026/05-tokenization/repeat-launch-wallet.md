## Finding WALLET-01

**Verdict:** CONFIRMED where wallet-to-token attribution is supported by primary platform or on-chain evidence  
**Evidence grade:** E3 for the wallet attribution proposition itself  
**Scope:** repeated launches from the same creator wallet among individually verified assets

The same creator wallet is associated with more than one identified AI/Grok-linked token launch.

This finding does not depend on a complete historical launch census.

## Finding WALLET-02

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED  
**Evidence grade:** E2  
**Scope:** repeated narrative tokenization among the identified and source-hardened subset

Multiple identified launches reference AI/Grok systems, technical projects, creator content or related public narratives.

This supports recurrence in the observed subset.

It does not establish how common the pattern is across the wallet's complete launch history.

## Finding WALLET-03

**Verdict:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Evidence grade:** E0 for prevalence estimation  
**Scope:** proportion of the wallet's full launch history fitting the pattern

The audit does not report a percentage of all launches that are AI/Grok-linked.

A complete independently validated denominator was not reconstructed, and discovery was topic-driven.

Any percentage would therefore be vulnerable to selection bias.

## Identity attribution

The profile label `ridarkdev`, RIDARK branding and related metadata make a public association plausible.

They do not establish that a named X account or real-world person controls the wallet.

The proposition that the wallet is controlled by `@ridark_eth` remains **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

This standard is deliberately consistent with the SPIDER and REAPER findings: metadata and token naming are not treated as identity proof when third-party tokenization is itself an observed mechanism.

## Not established

The repeat-wallet evidence does not establish:

- complete launch count
- percentage of AI/Grok-linked launches
- automated selection or deployment
- common control of referenced creator accounts
- creator authorization
- pre-launch coordination
- fraudulent intent

## Final conclusion

The wallet supports a repeated-launch observation in the source-verified subset.

It does not support a population-level prevalence claim, identity attribution, motive or a single coordinated-operation conclusion.
