## what this is about

audit how claims about autonomous trading performance spread and become connected to tradable tokens.

## why it is examined

distinguish observable asset movement, technical capability, claimed trading profit and creator-fee payouts.

## what is documented

evidence ledger version 1.0 is frozen. the audit preserves source captures, corrections, rejected hypotheses and limits on coordination claims.

## what remains uncertain

the claimed original trade was not independently reconstructed from the identified public evidence. failure to verify does not prove non-occurrence or a shared operator.

## research status

archived. this label describes the research work, not guilt, legal liability or the truth of every claim. source dates and evidence grades remain those in the linked records; this navigation audit adds no new findings.
