# SPIDER creator onboarding case

## Purpose

SPIDER is the strongest counterexample to the hypothesis that creator participation must exist before token launch.

## Token

Mint:

`6m6hkSNrZ1FxuLLdbcf9UAraPvUZMgfcCSsx3hh4pump`

## Creator statement

A captured post from `@phosphenq` stated, in substance, that seven spider bots ran a night shift, that somebody turned them into a coin overnight, that the creator did not deploy it and learned about it from a stranger in replies, and that creator fees had been routed to the creator.

The underlying creator narrative also referenced seven Grok bots operating on one computer and a low-cost overnight work setup.

## Public chronology

Captured Orbitalx0 posts referenced the same SPIDER mint and later described the creator as onboarded.

The creator subsequently replied with the same contract address.

This creates a public sequence consistent with:

1. third-party tokenization
2. creator discovery / claim
3. creator onboarding or adoption

## Finding SPIDER-01

**Verdict:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Evidence grade:** E1  
**Scope:** pre-launch creator participation

The available public evidence does not establish that the creator participated in the token launch before it occurred.

The creator's own public statement points in the opposite direction, but private pre-launch communication cannot be excluded.

## Finding SPIDER-02

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED  
**Evidence grade:** E2  
**Scope:** public post-launch onboarding chronology

Multiple posts from separate accounts referencing the same mint support a sequence in which creator association became public after the token already existed.

## Finding SPIDER-03

**Verdict:** UNVERIFIED  
**Evidence grade:** E1  
**Scope:** specific creator-fee payout

Public statements described fee routing and creator benefit.

The audit did not reconstruct the full mint → fee configuration → recipient mapping → claim transaction → recipient wallet path.

The payout therefore remains unverified at transaction level.

## Counterevidentiary significance

SPIDER weakened an earlier working hypothesis that creator-linked tokenization implied necessary pre-launch creator coordination.

The case instead demonstrates that the observed market mechanism can operate through third-party launch followed by creator onboarding.
