# REAPER creator onboarding case

## Purpose

REAPER is a second creator-onboarding case used to test whether the SPIDER chronology was isolated.

## Token

Mint:

`FRPV7Df98MRYLQus6LvJHkjWukUai9wonumScVcypump`

## Public narrative

Material identified during the audit attributed to `@0xkkai` described the token as having been launched by someone else as a sign of support, followed by the creator taking communications and continuing development.

Orbital-related posts later described the creator as having tapped in or claimed the token and referenced fee redirection / claim mechanics.

## Finding REAPER-01

**Verdict:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**Evidence grade:** E1  
**Scope:** creator participation from the outset

The available public material does not establish that the creator participated in the original token launch.

## Finding REAPER-02

**Verdict:** SUPPORTED, subject to final primary-source capture  
**Evidence grade:** E2 where same-mint chronology is independently documented  
**Scope:** post-launch creator onboarding

The identified chronology is consistent with token launch first and creator adoption later.

## Finding REAPER-03

**Verdict:** UNVERIFIED  
**Evidence grade:** E1  
**Scope:** specific creator-fee payout

Public claims about fee routing or claiming are not treated as transaction-level proof of payout.

## Source limitation

REAPER currently relies more heavily than SPIDER on archived and secondary discovery material.

For that reason, exact status IDs, original captures and same-mint chronology should be treated as `PENDING` until deposited and independently checked in the repository.

## Cross-case significance

Taken with SPIDER, REAPER supports the proposition that post-launch creator onboarding is observable in more than one case.

It does not establish that all creator-linked launches follow this sequence.
