# Timing analysis

## Purpose

Timing is used as a structural indicator only.

Temporal proximity is not treated as proof of automation, common control, prior knowledge or causal provenance.

## Final conclusion

Timing may identify cases worth deeper inspection.

It cannot independently establish:

- automation
- causal copying
- common control
- pre-launch coordination
- prior knowledge

The governing rule is:

**Temporal proximity ≠ causal provenance.**
