# Final Evidence Summary

**Evidence freeze:** 25 September 2026

Thirty claim families were screened from Benjamin Netanyahu's 24 September 2026 U.N. General Assembly speech.

Seven remain deliberately outside ordinary factual scoring because they are counterfactual, legal, superlative or definition-sensitive.

Among the remaining 23 adjudicable claim families:

| Evidence outcome | Claim families |
|---|---:|
| Core claim supported / mostly supported | 5 |
| Supported core with material qualification | 12 |
| Unsupported by available evidence | 4 |
| Contradicted by available evidence | 2 |

These counts are descriptive of the selected claim ledger. They are not an overall rating of Netanyahu, the speech, Israeli policy or any political choice.

## Structural finding

A recurring pattern survived the contradiction pass:

**documented fact → compressed attribution/scope → stronger conclusion**

Examples include:

- nuclear-facility damage → prevention of a future nuclear weapon;
- multi-actor polio campaign → Israel gave the vaccinations;
- multi-party hostage process → Israel and the U.S. brought everyone home;
- documented Qatar university funding → asserted political motive;
- rising antisemitic incidents → mayoral causation.

Working term:

## Evidence Compression

> **Evidence Compression occurs when several evidentiary steps — fact, attribution, causality, scope or legal conclusion — are compressed into a single statement, making the final claim stronger than the evidence directly establishes.**

This differs from the Traoré audit's **Narrative Inflation**.

Narrative Inflation described a claim growing through repeated online retelling.

Evidence Compression can occur inside a single political statement.

## What the audit establishes

- Several claims in the speech have strong factual cores.
- Several require material qualification because attribution, scope or causality exceeds the directly supported evidence.
- Some claims are unsupported or contradicted by the benchmarks reviewed.
- Some statements cannot be scored responsibly and remain on HOLD.
- No overall factual-accuracy score is assigned to the speaker or speech.

## What the audit does not establish

- deliberate deception or motive;
- a legal judgment on genocide;
- that every Israeli or Palestinian claim in the underlying conflicts is resolved;
- that one selected speech represents all statements by the speaker.
