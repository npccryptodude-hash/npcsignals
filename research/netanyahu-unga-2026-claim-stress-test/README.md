# Netanyahu UNGA 2026 Claim Stress Test

**NPCsignals Research**  
**Speech date:** 24 September 2026  
**Evidence freeze:** 25 September 2026  
**Status:** Second-pass adjudication

This archive tests falsifiable claims from Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's address to the 81st United Nations General Assembly.

The project does not assess whether Netanyahu is a good or bad political leader. It separates factual claims, interpretation, legal characterisation, causal claims and counterfactual rhetoric.

## Method

Claims are classified as:

- PASS / MOSTLY SUPPORTED
- MIXED / MISLEADING
- UNSUPPORTED
- CONTRADICTED
- HOLD / EXCLUDED

HOLD is used where a claim lacks a stable denominator, depends on inaccessible evidence, is primarily counterfactual, or requires a legal judgment rather than ordinary factual scoring.

## First-pass result

The speech contains several distinct claim types:

1. historically testable claims;
2. quantitative military and security claims;
3. claims with a documented factual core but stronger framing;
4. legal conclusions;
5. counterfactual statements;
6. claims that cannot yet be independently verified.

The audit therefore avoids a single speech-level "true/false" verdict.
