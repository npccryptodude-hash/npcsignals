# Source Notes

## Speech source

The United Nations General Debate archive records Benjamin Netanyahu's address at the 81st Session on 24 September 2026. A full transcript released by the Israeli Prime Minister's Office was also published by JNS.

## Iran nuclear facilities

Reuters reported in January 2026 that U.S. and Israeli strikes had severely affected Iran's declared enrichment facilities. Natanz's pilot plant was destroyed and underground sites at Natanz and Fordow were assessed as heavily damaged. The IAEA had not regained full access and Iran's enriched-uranium stockpile remained uncertain.

This supports major physical damage. It does not by itself prove the counterfactual that a nuclear weapon was thereby prevented.

## 1967

The U.S. State Department Office of the Historian records that Israel captured the West Bank, Gaza Strip, East Jerusalem and Golan Heights in the June 1967 war. It also records the pre-war crisis, Egypt's mobilization and closure of the Strait of Tiran, and that Israel initiated military action on 5 June.

## Settler violence

UN OCHA reported that as of 11 May 2026, 12 Palestinians had been killed by Israeli settlers in attacks during 2026, with additional disputed settler/force cases. By July, OCHA reported further settler-linked fatalities and more than 1,300 attacks causing casualties or property damage. By mid-September, more than 1,600 such attacks had been recorded in 2026.

This directly conflicts with a speech formulation limiting the death toll to two or three.

## Qatar and U.S. universities

The U.S. Department of Education reported more than $1.1 billion in reportable gifts and contracts from Qatar to U.S. universities in 2025 alone. The broader claim about Qatar funding U.S. media, and the asserted political motive, require separate evidence.

## Turkey and journalists

Reporters Without Borders ranked Türkiye 163rd of 180 in its 2026 World Press Freedom Index and reported three journalists imprisoned at the time due to their work. RSF has historically described Turkey as the world's biggest prison for journalists, but that historical description does not establish a current 'world record' in 2026.

## Northern Cyprus

UN Security Council Resolution 541 (1983) declared the Turkish Cypriot secession legally invalid. UN records and resolutions have repeatedly referred to foreign occupation and called for withdrawal of foreign forces. Legal wording should therefore be attributed to the relevant international framework rather than presented as an unqualified political slogan.

## Gaza aid diversion

Reuters reported in July 2025 that an internal USAID analysis of 156 aid-loss incidents from October 2023 through May 2025 found no evidence of systematic Hamas diversion of U.S.-funded humanitarian assistance. The analysis did not establish that diversion never occurred.

## Polio

WHO and UNICEF reported that roughly 559,000 children received the first polio dose in September 2024 and more than half a million received the second dose later that year. The campaign was coordinated by WHO, UNICEF, UNRWA, the Palestinian Ministry of Health and partners, with area-specific humanitarian pauses agreed to enable vaccination.

Thus, a cumulative figure exceeding one million administered doses is plausible, while attributing the vaccinations directly to Israel materially compresses the delivery chain.

## Second-pass resolutions — 25 September 2026

### Remaining hostages

Contemporary reporting states that the October 2025 ceasefire led to the release of all remaining living hostages and that the final hostage body was recovered in January 2026. The outcome is therefore real. Attribution solely to Israel and the United States is too narrow because Hamas, Qatar, Egypt and other mediators were part of the negotiating and implementation chain.

### Civilian-warning counts

Israeli government records report more than 15 million text messages, more than 12 million pre-recorded calls, more than 4.5 million leaflets and more than 45,000 individual calls by December 2023. This supports the speech's millions-level warning claim, while the audit preserves that these are principally Israeli self-reported operational totals.

### Gaza food tonnage

Israeli-published figures include more than two million tons of total aid in one cumulative series and approximately 1.78 million tons of food in a later ceasefire-period series. These are not one continuous, independently weighed dataset and cannot be mechanically combined. The claim therefore has a substantial factual core but compresses category, period and measurement differences.

### New York Jewish safety

NYPD-linked reporting for 2026 shows anti-Jewish hate crimes remained the largest hate-crime category and rose year over year in the first eight months. That supports a deterioration in one measurable safety indicator. It does not establish that Mayor Mamdani caused the increase.

### Holy Land Five

Mamdani released lyrics expressing support for the Holy Land Five. The five defendants had been convicted in federal court of offenses including providing material support to Hamas. The audit treats the factual core as supported while separating the existence of the lyric from broader political characterisations.

### Motaz Azaiza

Reporting documents that Mamdani hosted Gazan photojournalist Motaz Azaiza at Gracie Mansion. In a September 2026 interview, Azaiza said he filmed Israeli captives being beaten after their abduction on October 7 and deliberately withheld the footage because he feared Israel would identify and kill the Palestinians shown. This supports the factual core of the speech claim.

### Rama Duwaji social-media activity

Jewish Insider reported preserved social-media evidence showing that Rama Duwaji liked multiple posts that celebrated or positively framed the October 7 attack. The additional allegation involving a post saying Tel Aviv should not exist was not independently verified in this second pass and remains unconfirmed.

### Genocide legal status

The South Africa v. Israel genocide case remains pending before the International Court of Justice. In May 2026 the ICJ set deadlines extending written proceedings into 2027 and 2029. No final merits judgment existed as of the evidence freeze. The speech's genocide conclusion therefore remains outside the ordinary factual denominator.
