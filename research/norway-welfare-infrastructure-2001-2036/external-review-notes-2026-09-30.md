# External Review Notes — 2026-09-30

The following issues were raised during external review and are incorporated into the audit.

## PISA data quality

OECD reports an overall exclusion rate for Norway of **10.4% in PISA 2025**, up from **7.3% in 2022**. OECD states that the high exclusion rate may bias reported performance upwards.

Implication:

- the deterioration remains confirmed;
- exact mean levels require caution;
- this does not establish any causal role for school buildings.

## KOSTRA classification risk

The comparison between maintenance expenditure and gross building investment is descriptive, not a direct measurement of a maintenance gap.

KOSTRA accounting distinguishes:
- maintenance that preserves existing standard, normally in operating accounts;
- standard-enhancing additions / improvements, normally in investment accounts.

Major rehabilitation can therefore appear in investment expenditure where it extends useful life or raises standard.

Implication:

> **NOK 3.7bn maintenance vs. NOK 30.3bn gross investment demonstrates different accounting flows, but should not be interpreted as a simple 3.7-to-30.3 measure of lifecycle underfunding.**

The interpretation is marked **SUPPORTED / classification-sensitive**, not CONFIRMED as a maintenance-gap measure.

## RIF source incentive

RIF is the industry association for consulting engineering firms.

Its *Norges tilstand* estimates are useful technical evidence, but RIF is not a neutral government statistical agency and its members participate in markets that can benefit from infrastructure upgrading.

Implication:

- retain the estimates;
- label them **external industry estimates**;
- do not treat them as official budget requirements;
- seek triangulation when possible.

## National versus local scarcity

The earlier phrase “scarcity fits poorly” was too broad.

Revised formulation:

> **Aggregate national financial scarcity fits the evidence poorly as the primary explanation. Local and sector-specific scarcity can remain real because financial capacity, ownership and financing responsibility are distributed differently across the system.**

This avoids treating “structural” and “scarcity” as mutually exclusive explanations.

## Maintenance mechanism strength

Deferred maintenance remains an important mechanism, but should be labelled:

**SUPPORTED AS A MATERIAL CONTRIBUTOR**

—not treated as the demonstrated dominant explanation.
