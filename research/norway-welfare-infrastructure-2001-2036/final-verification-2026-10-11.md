## Consequence for the NOK 250bn figure

Previous interpretation:

> NOK 250bn sits near the lower edge of a full gross-scope scenario.

**Status: SUPERSEDED**

Current interpretation:

> **NOK 250bn is a modelled maximum incremental programme envelope, not full gross-scope cost.**

Its sufficiency depends on:

- subtraction of already-funded relevant investment;
- project prioritisation;
- service-model choices;
- alternative capacity solutions;
- actual project costs.

The exact incremental requirement remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## Opportunity-cost stress test — RETAINED

The saving-versus-public-capital section remains valid.

The audit does not assume public infrastructure generally outperforms GPFG returns.

It requires both sides of the opportunity cost to be considered:

- foregone financial return from spending;
- cost of delay and foregone public-asset value from postponing necessary investment.
