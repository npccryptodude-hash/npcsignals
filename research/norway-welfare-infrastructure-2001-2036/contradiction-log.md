# Contradiction Log

Freeze date: **2026-09-30**

This file records claims that were tested and either contradicted, weakened or left unestablished. They are retained so rejected narratives do not silently disappear from the audit trail.

## C-01 — “Norway stopped investing in public buildings”

**Status: CONTRADICTED as a general claim**

Statistics Norway reports more than **NOK 30.28 billion** in municipal gross building investment in 2025, plus **NOK 5.69 billion** in county building investment.

This does not prove investment is sufficient or optimally allocated.

It does contradict the broad claim that public-building investment has stopped.

Source:  
https://www.ssb.no/offentlig-sektor/offentlig-forvaltning/statistikk/eiendomsforvaltning-i-kommunesektoren

---

## C-02 — “Poor school buildings caused Norway’s PISA decline”

**Status: NOT ESTABLISHED**

PISA 2025 confirms substantial deterioration in mathematics, reading and science.

The research also documents infrastructure problems.

However, no national causal identification has been established between building condition and the PISA decline.

Two real problems do not automatically form a proven causal chain.

Source:  
https://www.udir.no/tall-og-forskning/finn-forskning/rapporter/2026/pisa-2025-kompetanse-naturfag-lesing-matematikk/

---

## C-03 — “All current backlog is deferred maintenance”

**Status: NOT SUPPORTED**

Deferred lifecycle maintenance appears to be an important contributor.

But current upgrade needs can also reflect:

- building age
- changed standards
- changed functions
- accessibility requirements
- technical modernisation
- capacity changes
- replacement decisions

The backlog should not be assigned to one cause.

---

## C-04 — “All planned hospital investment can be subtracted from the NOK 67bn hospital backlog”

**Status: CONTRADICTED**

Hospital investment portfolios combine:

- rehabilitation
- replacement
- expansion
- new capacity
- medical technology
- ICT
- structural reorganisation

Only project-level matching can identify how much of planned investment reduces the existing technical backlog.

---

## C-05 — “NOK 250bn is the documented cost”

**Status: NOT ESTABLISHED**

NOK 250 billion is a **maximum planning envelope** used for stress testing.

It includes possible restoration, future capacity, modernisation and contingency.

The exact incremental need remains unresolved.

> **Ceiling, not target.**

---

## C-06 — “All NOK 250bn would have to be new money”

**Status: CONTRADICTED**

Existing municipal investment, hospital projects and national support schemes already overlap with parts of the need.

The correct relationship is:

> **gross need − already-funded relevant investment = incremental need**

The unresolved issue is the size of the subtraction.

---

## C-07 — “Unused room below the fiscal-rule benchmark is a permanent funding source”

**Status: CONTRADICTED BY STRESS TEST**

Fund value, economic conditions and fiscal-policy requirements change over time.

A large fall in the GPFG can materially change the measured percentage of fund use.

The programme should therefore be treated as a long-term budget priority rather than as a claim on temporary percentage-point headroom.

Source:  
https://www.regjeringen.no/en/documents/meld.-st.-1-2025-2026/id3126584/?ch=3

---

## C-08 — “High total welfare spending proves physical assets are adequately maintained”

**Status: NOT SUPPORTED**

Operating expenditure, maintenance, rehabilitation, replacement and new construction are separate categories.

High spending on a service does not demonstrate adequate lifecycle preservation of the buildings used to deliver it.

---

## C-09 — “Restoring today’s elderly-care buildings solves the future capacity problem”

**Status: CONTRADICTED BY DEMOGRAPHIC EVIDENCE**

Statistics Norway projects that the 80+ population will more than double by 2050 and the 90+ population will nearly triple.

Restoration and capacity expansion therefore require separate ledgers.

Source:  
https://www.ssb.no/befolkning/befolkningsframskrivinger/artikler/nasjonale-befolkningsframskrivinger-2026

---

## C-10 — “One national NOK/m² maintenance rate can be treated as a universal requirement”

**Status: NOT ESTABLISHED**

Building types, technical systems, age, condition and use differ substantially.

A lifecycle-maintenance floor is supported as a principle.

One universal rate is not.
