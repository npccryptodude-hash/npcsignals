# Final Verification — 2026-11-04

## Purpose

Final post-NOU verification and research freeze before editorial drafting.

## Result

**Status: PASS WITH EXPLICIT UNRESOLVED BASELINE INPUTS**

The core structural finding survives.

The research no longer treats NOK 250bn as demonstrated need. It is retained only as an upper model scenario for incremental programme capacity.

## Frozen findings

### 1. Aggregate national financial scarcity

**SUPPORTED AS INSUFFICIENT PRIMARY EXPLANATION**

Norway's sovereign fiscal capacity is strong, but local and sector-specific scarcity remains real.

### 2. Technical backlog

RIF remains the principal external technical estimate:

- municipal/county buildings: **NOK 168bn**
- hospitals: **NOK 67bn**

These are **external industry estimates**, not official budget requirements.

### 3. Hospital baseline

Regional health-authority plans show:

- total planned investment 2027–2030: **NOK 93.61bn**
- project/building categories before internal scope allocation: **NOK 74.96bn**
- Tier 1 high-confidence rehabilitation candidate: **NOK 0.611bn**
- Tier 2 mixed rehabilitation/capacity candidate frames: **NOK 3.722bn**

The hospital backlog cannot be added to planned investment as a separate bill, and the planned investment cannot be netted wholesale against the backlog estimate.

### 4. School and institution KOSTRA baseline

SSB confirms the relevant functions:

- **222 School premises**
- **261 Institution premises**

The correct property-management source is table 12559, and SSB documents corrections and classification effects affecting interpretation.

A complete reproducible EAK-Landet 2021–2025 series for both functions was not extracted in the current research environment.

**Status: UNRESOLVED BUT EXPLICITLY BOUNDED.**

No proxy has been inserted.

### 5. Elderly-care capacity

Husbanken commitments 2021–2025:

> **5,002 grant-supported places**

Average:

> **~1,000 places/year**

These include both renewal and growth.

SSB institution capacity increased only from **39,305 in 2021 to 39,915 in 2025**.

Updated SSB projection:

> **~22,800 additional round-the-clock places from 2024 to 2035**

Mechanical average:

> **~2,073 net places/year**

**Status: STRUCTURAL CAPACITY GAP — SUPPORTED.**

This does not establish that 22,800 traditional nursing-home places must be built.

### 6. Labour constraint

NOU 2026:12 materially strengthens the labour-capacity finding.

Projected health-trained workforce gap around 2040:

> **~42,000 FTEs**

**Status: STRONGLY SUPPORTED CORE CONSTRAINT.**

### 7. Organisational capacity

NOU 2026:12 supports the conclusion that municipal capacity, competence and organisational robustness are independent constraints.

**Status: SUPPORTED.**

## Programme model

The frozen model requires four gates:

1. baseline — would the investment happen anyway?
2. labour — can it be staffed sustainably?
3. organisation — can the owner plan, procure and operate it?
4. alternative solution — can the same need be met more effectively another way?

## Gross and incremental scale

Gross physical/demographic scale used for stress testing:

> **NOK 269–313bn**

This is **not** a net need estimate.

A deliberately conservative pre-KOSTRA sensitivity applies zero deduction for unresolved school/institution baselines and tests only:
- limited hospital overlap;
- modelled care-capacity baseline.

Result:

> **provisional pre-KOSTRA incremental range: approximately NOK 213–290bn**

Modelled midpoint:

> **~NOK 252bn**

This range is **MODELLED**, not a factual requirement.

It is expected to move lower if ordinary school/institution baseline investment and alternative service models are quantified.

## NOK 250bn classification

**as demonstrated need:** NOT ESTABLISHED  
**as gross full-scope cost:** SUPERSEDED  
**as spending target:** REJECTED  
**as retained maximum incremental model scenario:** RETAINED

## Publication-safe claims

The following claims are ready for editorial use:

- aggregate national financial scarcity is not a sufficient primary explanation;
- large investment and technical backlog can coexist;
- restoration and future capacity are separate problems;
- workforce capacity is a hard constraint;
- organisational capacity matters independently of money;
- ordinary planned investment must be subtracted by scope before incremental need is claimed;
- historical care-capacity growth is too weak to assume future demographic demand will be met automatically;
- NOK 250bn is a model ceiling, not a documented need.

## Claims that remain prohibited

Do not state that:

- Norway needs NOK 250bn;
- NOK 269–313bn is the net programme requirement;
- NOK 213–290bn is a factual need estimate;
- the full RIF hospital estimate is unfunded;
- RHF plans eliminate the hospital backlog;
- 22,800 traditional nursing-home places must be built;
- school-building causality explains PISA decline;
- KOSTRA 222/261 national baseline has been fully quantified.
