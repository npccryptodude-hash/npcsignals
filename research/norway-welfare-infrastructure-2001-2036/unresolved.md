# Unresolved Questions

Freeze date: **2026-10-11**

These items are intentionally left open rather than filled with weak estimates.

## 1. Exact incremental funding requirement

The updated gross-scope model is approximately **NOK 269–313 billion** before baseline subtraction, using the 2026 SSB projection of about 22,800 additional round-the-clock care places from 2024 to 2035.

That is not the amount of new funding required.

The final incremental requirement depends on:

- existing municipal investment
- already-approved hospital projects
- existing state support schemes
- which care-capacity projects would happen anyway
- project timing

The final size should only be set after:

> **gross need − already-funded relevant investment = incremental need**

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

---

## 2. Hospital baseline overlap

Current hospital investment plans contain a mixture of:

- rehabilitation
- replacement
- expansion
- new capacity
- technical modernisation
- ICT and equipment
- structural reorganisation

The NOK 67 billion RIF estimate cannot be reduced one-for-one by total planned hospital investment.

Project-level matching is still required.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

---

## 3. School baseline overlap

Existing school-investment schemes do not map cleanly onto the derived school-restoration range.

Some investment:
- reduces physical backlog,
- some adds capacity,
- some changes function,
- some would occur without the programme.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

---

## 4. Elderly-care capacity mix

The projected need for additional round-the-clock capacity does not specify one physical solution.

Future demand may be met through different combinations of:

- nursing homes
- staffed care housing
- home-based services
- technology
- service redesign

The 22,800-place calculation is therefore a **scale test**, not a construction forecast. The earlier 18,000-place estimate is **SUPERSEDED**.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

---

## 5. Lifecycle-maintenance standard

The research supports a funded lifecycle-maintenance floor.

It does not support one universal NOK/m² figure for:

- schools
- hospitals
- nursing homes
- offices
- specialist buildings

Different asset types require different standards.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

---

## 6. KOSTRA classification effect

The observed difference between maintenance expenditure and gross building investment does not directly reveal how much physical preservation occurs.

Major rehabilitation and standard-enhancing work may be classified as investment.

The size of this classification effect on the historical maintenance interpretation has not been quantified.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

---

## 7. Independent triangulation of RIF upgrade estimates

RIF provides the most useful national technical estimates located in this research, but is an industry association.

Independent national estimates with equivalent coverage would materially strengthen the audit.

**Status: UNRESOLVED / DESIRABLE VALIDATION**

---

## 8. Macroeconomic execution capacity

The programme's scale is now quantified relative to:
- trend mainland GDP,
- structural non-oil deficit,
- modelled direct-state capital.

What remains unresolved is the actual capacity to execute at a given annual pace without creating unacceptable pressure on:

- construction prices
- specialist labour
- wages
- interest rates

This requires future macro conditions and sector-capacity data.

**Status: CONDITION-DEPENDENT**

---

## 9. Final programme size

NOK 250 billion remains a modelled maximum **incremental** programme envelope.

The updated gross-scope range now exceeds NOK 250 billion before baseline subtraction. The envelope is therefore only potentially sufficient after subtracting already-funded relevant investment, applying project prioritisation and accounting for alternative care-capacity solutions.

The correct final programme size remains dependent on the 2027 asset audit and baseline subtraction.

**Status: MODEL DEPENDENT**
