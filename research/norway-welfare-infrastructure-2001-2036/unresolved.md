# Unresolved Questions

These items are intentionally left open rather than filled with weak estimates.

## 1. Exact incremental funding requirement

The gross technical need is not the same as the amount of new funding required.

Existing projects and support schemes overlap with parts of the need.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

## 2. Hospital baseline overlap

Current hospital investment plans contain a mixture of:

- rehabilitation
- replacement
- expansion
- new capacity
- technical modernisation
- ICT and equipment

The NOK 67 billion RIF backlog cannot be reduced one-for-one by total planned hospital investment.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

## 3. School baseline overlap

Existing school investment programmes do not map cleanly onto the physical restoration estimate.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

## 4. Elderly-care capacity mix

The future requirement can be met through different combinations of:

- nursing homes
- staffed care housing
- home-based services
- technology
- service redesign

The correct national mix is not fixed by this research.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

## 5. Lifecycle-maintenance standard

The research supports the need for a funded maintenance floor.

It does not support one universal NOK/m² figure for every building category.

**Status: UNRESOLVED**

## 6. Final programme size

NOK 250 billion remains a maximum planning envelope.

The final size should only be set after:

> **gross need − already-funded relevant investment = incremental need**

**Status: MODEL DEPENDENT**
