# Methodology

## Scope

This project examines physical welfare infrastructure in three sectors:

- schools
- hospitals
- elderly care

It does not treat foreign aid as a competing funding pot and does not attempt to attribute responsibility to individual political parties.

The purpose is to test system-level explanations and financing capacity, not to recommend a political party or electoral choice.

## Time structure

### 2001–2025 — Historical evidence
How did national financial capacity, public spending, maintenance and physical welfare infrastructure develop?

### 2027–2036 — Programme period
What would a realistic ten-year restoration and capacity programme require?

### 2037–2050 — Preservation period
Can the restored assets be maintained without recreating the backlog?

## Four-ledger rule

The analysis keeps four ledgers separate:

1. **Existing Asset Restoration**
2. **Already Funded Baseline**
3. **Future Capacity**
4. **Lifecycle Maintenance**

This prevents gross technical need from being confused with new funding need and prevents demographic expansion from being labelled as maintenance backlog.

The central accounting identity is:

> **gross need − already-funded relevant investment = incremental need**

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Primary documents and raw data
2. Official records and direct statements
3. Peer-reviewed or methodologically transparent research
4. High-quality secondary reporting
5. Specialist / industry analysis
6. Social-media claims and commentary

## Source-context rule

Source quality and source incentives are treated separately.

A technically useful source can still have an institutional or commercial interest.

Example:

RIF's *Norges tilstand* is used as technical evidence, but RIF is an industry association for consulting engineering firms. Its figures are therefore labelled **external industry estimates**, not official government requirements.

## Accounting-classification rule

Observed accounting categories are not automatically physical-condition measures.

In KOSTRA:

- maintenance that preserves existing standard is normally classified as operating expenditure;
- standard-enhancing improvements and some major rehabilitation can be classified as investment.

Therefore:

> **maintenance expenditure vs. gross investment is descriptive, not a direct measure of the maintenance gap.**

Any causal interpretation based on accounting categories must be separately justified.

## PISA data-quality rule

PISA results are treated as educational-outcome evidence, not infrastructure evidence.

For PISA 2025, OECD reports a Norwegian overall exclusion rate of **10.4%**, above the PISA standard tolerance and higher than in 2022.

The outcome decline remains usable as a trend signal, but exact score levels carry this data-quality caveat.

No causal link between school-building condition and national PISA performance is assumed.

## Analytical safeguards

- investment is not the same as maintenance
- gross need is not the same as incremental need
- national financial capacity is not the same as local fiscal capacity
- correlation is not causation
- demographic expansion is not maintenance backlog
- existing funded projects must be matched before subtraction
- model assumptions must be labelled
- unresolved questions remain unresolved
- rejected claims remain visible in the contradiction log

## Programme-model rule

NOK 250 billion is not treated as an observed requirement.

The ceiling is tested against a derived gross-scope range and macroeconomic scale.

Current derivation:

- gross restoration core: **NOK 167–192bn**
- future care-capacity scale test: **NOK 76.9–87.9bn**
- equipment / technical reserve: **NOK 5–10bn**
- combined gross scope: **approximately NOK 249–290bn**

This is before baseline subtraction.

## Evidence labels

### CONFIRMED
Directly supported by a reliable source.

### SUPPORTED
Multiple pieces of evidence point in the same direction, but the claim is broader than a directly observed statistic.

### DERIVED
Calculated reproducibly from sourced data.

### MODELLED
A scenario or planning assumption used to test feasibility.

### UNRESOLVED
Evidence is insufficient to close the question reliably.

### CONTRADICTED / NOT ESTABLISHED
Available evidence conflicts with the claim, or the proposed causal relationship has not been demonstrated.

## Update discipline

No figure should silently replace an earlier figure.

Future updates should record:

> **previous estimate → new estimate → reason for revision**

Every material update should record:

- freeze date
- source publication date
- observed / derived / modelled status
- any definition change
- any source-context change
- whether the update changes a finding or only a numerical estimate
