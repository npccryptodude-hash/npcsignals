## what this is about

reconstruct the token launch, selling, promotional claims and observable movement of funds.

## why it is examined

allow readers to distinguish transaction structure, gross receipts and ownership attribution.

## what is documented

the preserved main report records 17 case-relevant sellers and 35 sales. later records examine launch funding and joint transaction authorization. these are separately dated evidence layers.

## what remains uncertain

common beneficial ownership, a final beneficiary, intent and a definitive human-control link remain unresolved. gross receipts are not net profit.

## research status

ongoing investigation. this label describes the research work, not guilt, legal liability or the truth of every claim. source dates and evidence grades remain those in the linked records; this navigation audit adds no new findings.
