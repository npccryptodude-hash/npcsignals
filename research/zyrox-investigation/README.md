## Evidence standards

- **CONFIRMED:** directly verified facts at their defined scope, including explicitly identified conservative accounting deductions.
- **SUPPORTED:** explanations supported by multiple concrete observations without conclusive proof, including the broader coordinated operational structure.
- **UNRESOLVED:** questions the available evidence cannot settle, including operator identity, common ownership, final beneficiary and intent.

Mixed balances prevent automatic attribution of later payments. Re-established lower bounds remain within mixed principal; co-signing and routing do not establish ownership. The internal verification appendix remains part of the locked evidence copy, not the reader-facing Medium article.
