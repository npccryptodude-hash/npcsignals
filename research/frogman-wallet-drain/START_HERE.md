## what this is about

reconstruct publicly documented transfers associated with the reported wallet drain, across solana and evm networks.

## why it is examined

make the observed money routes, account credits and limits independently inspectable.

## what is documented

the november audit reconciles 35 listed accounts and one separately reported additional account. the 35-account baseline and the additional account must remain separate before any combined total is used.

## what remains uncertain

human control, compromise method, complete victim-loss allocation and native monero settlement remain unresolved.

## research status

ongoing investigation. this label describes the research work, not guilt, legal liability or the truth of every claim. source dates and evidence grades remain those in the linked records; this navigation audit adds no new findings.
