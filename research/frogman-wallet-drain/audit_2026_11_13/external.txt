        <p>After selling the tokens on Meteora, the receiver put 13,241 SOL into Privacy Cash in ten deposits. That is 96% of what went in, out in the same half hour.</p>

        <p class="ev">Evidence: e.g. bridge 7dAqvMMyA9Ne… → 0x3b80…a65c, fill 0xb142f40104…</p></article>

Ten of those branches each funded a fresh wallet that sent its ETH through LI.FI and received USDC on Arbitrum.</p>
        <p class="ev">Evidence: Collector outflow 0x34fa1689d4…; LI.FI 0x1ccc5f8252…</p></article>

Together they hold 3,586.8 XMR1, about $2M. None had been redeemed at our last check. Wagyu controls that redemption, which makes it the one place where this money can still be stopped.</p>

    <p class="lede">Solana transactions and balances, Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum transfers, Mayan swap records and Hyperliquid's public info API (ledger, balances, fills), read by the Onchain Files engine. The Privacy Cash link is an amount and timing match and is marked Inferred. The victim appears only through his own public posts and the wallets the trackers published; no other personal details are used.</p>
