# Methodology

## Scope

The audit examines public claims and narratives about Ibrahim Traoré and Burkina Faso, with emphasis on economic performance, agriculture, industrialisation, mining, infrastructure, education, salaries, governance and synthetic media.

## Classification

Claims are classified as:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | Evidence directly supports the claim |
| Partially supported | A documented core exists, but the claim overstates or alters it |
| Unverified | Available evidence is insufficient |
| Contradicted | Available evidence directly conflicts with the claim |
| Unresolved | Evidence is incomplete or materially inconsistent |

## Direction coding

Directional social content is coded as:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PRO | Reinforces Traoré, his achievements, or a victim/anti-imperialist narrative |
| ANTI | Weakens Traoré or attributes a negative event/claim to him |
| NEUTRAL | Primarily factual or descriptive |
| AMB | Direction cannot be assigned reliably |

Direction and factual accuracy are coded separately.

A pro-Traoré post can be true.  
An anti-Traoré post can be true.  
Direction is not a truth classification.

## Sampling stages

### Calibration Sample

A first sample of already documented false or misleading claims was used to identify recurring mechanisms and develop coding categories.

This sample is **not population-representative**, because fact-checking organisations select which claims they investigate.

### Main Sample

Fifty identifiable public posts or videos were coded for:

- direction
- factual status
- platform
- AI/manipulation
- narrative cluster

Closely related reposts were later grouped into narrative clusters.

### Validation Sample

A separate set of fifty items was collected using general public discovery rather than fact-checking organisations as the starting point.

The purpose was to test whether directional asymmetry survived outside the fact-check corpus.

## Cluster adjustment

Posts derived from the same underlying narrative are not treated as fully independent inventions.

Examples include repeated:

- tax-free claims
- free-education claims
- foreign-rally footage
- protest footage
- death rumours

Cluster-adjusted analysis therefore reports both raw items and independent narrative clusters.

## Freeze rule

Once a sample or ledger is frozen:

- classifications are not silently rewritten
- later evidence may create an annotation
- later evidence may create a revision note
- sensitivity analysis may be added
- the original frozen record remains preserved

## Evidence hierarchy

| Tier | Source type |
|---|---|
| A | Primary documentary evidence: laws, budgets, official project records, original speeches, IMF/World Bank datasets |
| B | Independent high-quality reporting/research |
| C | Specialist verification and fact-checking |
| D | Original social-media evidence |
| E | Secondary aggregation and reposts |

Repeated Tier E claims do not become stronger evidence simply through distribution.

## Limitations

The audit does not establish:

- the percentage of all online content that is pro- or anti-Traoré
- the percentage of all misinformation that supports Traoré
- a single actor controlling the broader information environment
- government authorship of synthetic narratives
- population-level belief in the narratives

Platform search accessibility also varies, which limits uniform sampling across services.
