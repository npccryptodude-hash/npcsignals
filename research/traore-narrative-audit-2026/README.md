# Ibrahim Traoré Narrative Audit — 2026

**NPCsignals Research**

**Evidence freeze:** 25 September 2026  
**Status:** Research archive / pre-publication

This repository contains the evidence trail behind an NPCsignals investigation into the gap between documented developments in Burkina Faso under Ibrahim Traoré and claims circulating online.

The project does **not** evaluate Ibrahim Traoré as a political leader. It tests whether specific claims exceed the strength of the evidence supporting them.

## Research question

> Where does the documented record end, and where does the online narrative begin to exceed the evidence?

## Research structure

- Initial claim ledger
- Calibration sample
- Main sample
- Validation sample
- Cluster adjustment
- Contradiction pass
- Source ledger
- Final adjudication

## Core concept

**Narrative Inflation** is the progressive transformation of a documented event, policy, ambition or achievement into a claim stronger than the underlying evidence supports.

Observed pathway:

```
REALITY
  ↓
INTERPRETATION
  ↓
AMPLIFICATION
  ↓
NARRATIVE INFLATION
  ↓
SYNTHETIC MYTHMAKING
```

The critical transition occurs when:

> **claim strength exceeds evidence strength**

## Sampling note

Across the project, **128 raw observations** were recorded before deduplication and narrative clustering.

These samples are not random population samples of Facebook, TikTok, X, YouTube or the internet as a whole. The results identify patterns in the observed material but must not be interpreted as population-wide prevalence estimates.

## Research principle

**Evidence over velocity.**

A documented achievement does not validate every narrative built around it.

Misinformation surrounding a real achievement does not erase the achievement.
