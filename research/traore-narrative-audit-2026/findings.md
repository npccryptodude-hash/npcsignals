# Frozen Findings

**Freeze date: 25 September 2026**

## Finding 1 — Documented record

Multiple reforms, investments and projects attributed to the Traoré period have a verifiable factual core, including agricultural mechanisation, industrial projects, increased state involvement in mining and major infrastructure projects.

**Status:** High confidence.

## Finding 2 — Narrative Inflation

A recurring mechanism transforms a documented event, policy, ambition or project into a stronger claim than the evidence supports.

Common subtypes include:

- outcome inflation
- scope inflation
- timeline inflation
- completion inflation
- attribution inflation

**Status:** Strong observed signal.

## Finding 3 — Synthetic Mythmaking

A separate layer consists of events, speeches, endorsements or supporting material that did not occur and were created through AI, manipulated audio/video or fabricated narratives.

**Status:** Confirmed.

## Finding 4 — Context substitution

Authentic footage from another place or event is sometimes relabelled as Traoré-related evidence.

This mechanism can operate in both pro- and anti-Traoré directions.

**Status:** Confirmed.

## Finding 5 — Directional asymmetry

Across the calibration, main and validation samples, clearly directional material was strongly skewed toward pro-Traoré framing.

**Status:** Replicated in tested samples.

**Limitation:** Not population-representative.

## Finding 6 — Amplification networks

Specific networks displaying suspiciously similar behaviour and AI-assisted distribution have been documented.

**Status:** Supported in specific networks.

**Not established:** A single controlling actor for the broader ecosystem.

## Core signal

> **The documented record and the mythology are not opposites. The mythology often works because a documented record exists underneath it.**

The signal begins where:

> **claim strength exceeds evidence strength**

## Non-findings

The research does not establish that:

- Ibrahim Traoré personally creates false content
- the Burkina Faso government controls the broader misinformation ecosystem
- Russia controls all pro-Traoré amplification
- the sample percentages describe the whole internet
- misinformation invalidates documented reforms
- documented reforms validate synthetic or inflated claims
