## Interpretation

The directional asymmetry replicated across all three tested designs.

This does **not** justify the statement that roughly 88–90% of all online content, or all misinformation, is pro-Traoré.

The supported statement is narrower:

> Across every sample tested in this investigation, clearly directional material was strongly skewed toward pro-Traoré framing.
