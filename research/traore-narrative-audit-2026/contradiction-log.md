# Contradiction Log

This file preserves source conflicts and research caveats rather than silently harmonising them.

## C-01 — 2025 fiscal deficit

**Issue:** IMF and World Bank reporting for Burkina Faso's 2025 fiscal deficit does not match exactly.

**Treatment:** Preserve the disagreement. Do not select one value merely because it produces a cleaner narrative.

**Status:** Open methodological note.

## C-02 — Gold refinery timeline

**Issue:** The national refinery project was launched with an original timetable of roughly eleven months.

**Treatment:** Project launch is Confirmed. Completion within the original timetable remains Unresolved unless directly demonstrated by later evidence.

## C-03 — Minister and public-sector salaries

**Issue:** Viral posts frequently combine three claims:
1. Traoré retained his captain salary
2. ministers received a 30% cut
3. public-sector workers received a 50% raise

**Treatment:** Adjudicate separately.

- Captain salary: Confirmed
- Minister remuneration reduction: Real, but viral percentage is unreliable
- Public-sector +50%: Contradicted / unsupported by administrative evidence

## C-04 — Mining nationalisation

**Issue:** Increased state ownership/control is frequently rewritten as full nationalisation.

**Treatment:** Describe specific state acquisitions and legal ownership provisions. Do not use "the entire mining sector was nationalised."

## C-05 — Economic attribution

**Issue:** Positive macroeconomic indicators are frequently attributed solely to Traoré.

**Treatment:** Record the improvement but separate it from causal attribution. Agriculture, mining, gold prices, weather, services and public policy may all contribute.
