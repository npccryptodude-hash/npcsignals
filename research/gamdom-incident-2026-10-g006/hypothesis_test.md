## Behavioral conclusion
The October 13 transfers represent first ordinary sender use for A on Polygon (nonces 0/1), preceded by B's first ordinary Polygon transaction funding A. B also first displays ordinary sender use on Ethereum that day, including a dated type-4 delegation. This is a material activation pattern in the observed history. It cannot be assigned a statistical anomaly score because a comparable active baseline is absent. No claim of theft, guilt or customer loss follows.
