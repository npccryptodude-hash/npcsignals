# democratic representation and governing power

[all research](../INDEX.md) · [how to read evidence](../READING_GUIDE.md)

**research status: ongoing research**

## what this is about

test how political voice, voter preferences, governing power and institutional restraint interact in a fixed 15-country sample.

## why it is examined

show the methods and case-selection decisions behind a comparative exploratory instrument.

## what is documented

the archive preserves a frozen framework, a resolved 90-case design, source audits and corrections. a complete design does not mean all results are final.

## what remains uncertain

scoring and certification conditions in the dated records remain applicable. this is not a representative global democracy ranking or a settled causal model.

## research status

ongoing research. this label describes the research work, not guilt, legal liability or the truth of every claim. source dates and evidence grades remain those in the linked records; this navigation audit adds no new findings.
