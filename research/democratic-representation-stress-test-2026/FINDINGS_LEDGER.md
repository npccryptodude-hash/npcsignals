NPCsignals — Cross-Dimensional Findings Ledger v1.0

Status note

This ledger separates findings that are already supported from mechanisms that remain candidates. Association is not treated as causation.

CDF-01 — Representation–Voice Gap

Claim
Formal electoral representation can remain strong while citizens perceive substantially less influence over government decision-making between elections.

Evidence strength
HIGH

Status
SUPPORTED

Falsification condition
The finding weakens if harmonized longitudinal data show that perceived political voice normally tracks formal representation closely or if the apparent gap is primarily a measurement artefact.

CDF-02 — Political Voice ↔ Political Trust

Claim
Citizens who perceive meaningful political voice exhibit substantially higher political trust than citizens who feel they lack influence.

Evidence strength
VERY HIGH for association
UNRESOLVED for causality

Status
SUPPORTED ASSOCIATION / CAUSAL DIRECTION UNRESOLVED

Falsification condition
The proposed mechanism weakens if substantial changes in perceived voice are not accompanied by corresponding changes in political trust over time.

CDF-03 — Realignment Is Not the Failure Mode

Claim
Large electoral realignment is compatible with both democratic resilience and democratic stress. Political movement toward another ideological bloc is not itself evidence of democratic deterioration.

Evidence strength
HIGH

Status
SUPPORTED

Falsification condition
The finding weakens if larger comparative samples show that electoral realignment independently predicts institutional deterioration after controlling for translation and restraint mechanisms.

CDF-04 — Information Stress Is Mechanism-Specific

Claim
Information-integrity stress does not constitute one common pathway across democracies.

Observed mechanisms
- covert partisan communication
- coordinated foreign influence
- persistent corrected falsehoods in an open media system
- political pressure on public-service media
- legal restriction of political expression

Evidence strength
MODERATE–HIGH

Status
SUPPORTED DESCRIPTIVE FINDING / CAUSAL ROLE UNRESOLVED

Falsification condition
The typology weakens if the apparently different mechanisms turn out to share the same dominant causal structure and measurable political effects.

CDF-05 — Stress Frequency ≠ Corrective Capacity

Claim
The frequency with which governments test institutional boundaries and the capacity of institutions to correct those actions are separate democratic properties.

Evidence strength
MODERATE–HIGH

Status
SUPPORTED TYPOLOGY

Falsification condition
The distinction weakens if apparent corrective mechanisms are mostly symbolic and fail to alter policy, power, or implementation in practice.

CDF-06 — Dissatisfaction ≠ Rejection of Democracy

Claim
Low satisfaction with current political institutions or leaders should not automatically be interpreted as rejection of democratic government.

Evidence strength
MODERATE–HIGH

Status
SUPPORTED DISTINCTION

Falsification condition
The distinction weakens if declining institutional trust is consistently followed by declining support for democratic norms and increased support for non-democratic alternatives.

CDF-07 v0.2 — Transmission Capacity Under Realignment

Claim
The institutional mechanisms that translate voter preference into governing power are often long-standing. Their democratic consequences become more visible when electoral realignment grows large enough to stress those mechanisms.

Historical revision
An earlier formulation implied that a new preference-to-power problem may have emerged recently. Historical comparison of Japan, the United Kingdom, Germany, the United States, Botswana, and Thailand partly falsified that interpretation.

The revised model treats electoral realignment as a stress load that can reveal long-standing properties of the transmission system.

Observed outcome types

ABSORPTION
Changing preferences translate into governing influence while ordinary restraints remain effective.

DELAYED ABSORPTION
Transmission is initially distorted or delayed but eventual turnover occurs through ordinary democratic mechanisms.

BOTTLENECK
Representation increases substantially while governing translation remains much weaker.

OVERRIDE / RESTRAINT STRESS
Either electoral mandate ceases to reliably determine who can govern, or elected power increasingly attempts to escape ordinary democratic restraint.

Evidence strength
MODERATE / CONVERGING

Status
PRIMARY CANDIDATE / HISTORICALLY SUPPORTED / CAUSAL STATUS UNRESOLVED

Falsification conditions
CDF-07 should be weakened or rejected if:

1. weak governing translation does not correspond to lower voice or trust over time;
2. strong governing translation produces no observable difference in voice or trust;
3. electoral realignment does not make transmission mechanisms more politically consequential;
4. another variable explains the country differences substantially better;
5. 2026–2028 observations systematically move opposite to the framework's expectations.

Current project rule

No candidate finding is promoted to a signal solely because multiple countries appear to fit it. Counterexamples, longitudinal evidence, and falsification tests remain required.
