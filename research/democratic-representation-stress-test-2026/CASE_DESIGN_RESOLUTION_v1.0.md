This document does not score cases. It records which ledger rows are retained, narrowed, replaced or split before the final 90-case freeze.

## Resolution rules

1. A case must represent one bounded political event or policy process.
2. Two rows from the same country must not primarily score the same underlying event.
3. Electoral / Power Translation cases must test how electoral or parliamentary support converts into governing authority, representation or confidence.
4. Institutional / High Persistence cases must concern durable institutional architecture, constitutional rules, judicial structure, security architecture or similarly persistent state design.
5. Proposed reforms may be included only at the stage actually reached by the observation cutoff; they are not scored as enacted unless enacted.
6. Older contextual events remain background only unless the qualifying event itself falls within the contemporary window.

## Sweden

### Replace
OLD: SWE-2024-EP-01 — legal-gender reform / cross-bloc majority.

NEW: SWE-2024-EP-01 — January 2024 parliamentary no-confidence vote against Climate and Environment Minister Romina Pourmokhtari.

Reason: The legal-gender case is substantively a policy-legislation case and has weak category fit for Electoral / Power Translation. The no-confidence vote directly tests parliamentary confidence and the capacity of elected representatives to remove or retain executive officeholders.

Bounded event: Riksdag no-confidence vote, 17 January 2024. The motion failed: 66 in favour, 172 against, 104 abstentions.

Status: READY.

SWE-2026-EP-02 remains the 2026 general-election power-translation case, pending formal certification.

## United Kingdom

### Replace
OLD: GBR-2025-EP-02 — British Steel emergency intervention.

NEW: GBR-2025-EP-02 — 2025 English local elections and Runcorn & Helsby parliamentary by-election as challenger-to-power translation.

Reason: British Steel is an emergency legislative / major-policy case, not an electoral-power case. The May 2025 elections provide a clean test of changing voter preference translating into actual elected office and local governing control.

Bounded event: May 2025 local elections plus the Runcorn & Helsby by-election. Reform UK won the parliamentary seat, the Greater Lincolnshire mayoralty and control of multiple councils, including Staffordshire and Durham in early declared results.

Status: READY.

British Steel is removed from the six-case MIS core and may remain a reserve process case.

## France

### De-duplicate electoral/power cases
OLD FRA-2024-EP-01 — Barnier / 49.3 / no-confidence.
OLD FRA-2024-EP-02 — 2024 snap election / weakened presidential bloc / minority government.

Problem: Both rows largely score the same 2024 election-to-Barnier-collapse chain.

RETAIN / NARROW:
FRA-2024-EP-01 — 2024 snap election and fragmented National Assembly as electoral power translation.

REPLACE SECOND SLOT:
FRA-2025-EP-02 — Bayrou government loses parliamentary confidence vote, 8 September 2025.

Reason: The 2025 confidence vote is a separate government, separate year and separate parliamentary test. It measures whether a minority executive can remain in office without Assembly support.

Status: READY.

Barnier / 49.3 remains contextual evidence and should not be double-weighted as a separate EP row because budget-procedure stress is already represented in the fiscal-policy cases.

## Costa Rica

### Major-policy de-duplication
OLD CRI-2026-MP-01 — security / foreign-forces restraint.
OLD CRI-2026-MP-02 — security implementation.

Problem: Both rows are one security-policy family.

RETAIN / NARROW:
CRI-2026-MP-01 — hardline security legislative package and implementation, including June 2026 anti-crime bills and executive security measures.

REPLACE SECOND MAJOR-POLICY SLOT:
CRI-2026-MP-02 — electricity-market harmonisation / investment and infrastructure reform agenda submitted to the Legislative Assembly in May 2026.

Bounded subject: the electricity harmonisation initiative intended to introduce more competition and lower tariffs. Score only the legislative stage reached by cutoff, not an assumed enactment.

Status: READY WITH STAGE-LIMIT.

### Institutional de-duplication
RETAIN:
CRI-2026-INST-01 — proposal to shift Attorney General appointment power and reduce judicial budget autonomy.

REPLACE:
OLD CRI-2026-INST-02 — generic constitutional supermajority constraint.

Reason: This is a distinct institutional architecture proposal from Attorney General appointment reform. Score only the proposal / legislative stage actually reached.

Status: READY WITH STAGE-LIMIT.

## Botswana

## Japan

### Replace category-mismatched institutional row
OLD: JPN-2026-INST-01 — enlarged governing majority / supermajority.

NEW: JPN-2026-INST-01 — security architecture overhaul and accelerated defence build-up.

Bounded event: 2026 post-election security programme: accelerated defence spending to 2% of GDP, revision of the three core security documents, review of defence-export rules, and planned national intelligence council.

Reason: A parliamentary supermajority is an electoral outcome, not itself an institutional/high-persistence case. The security architecture overhaul is a genuine long-horizon state-design case.

Status: READY.

JPN-2025/40-INST-02 — nuclear revival / 2040 energy strategy remains distinct and READY.

### Fiscal row tightening
JPN-2026-MP-01 must not score the entire 'Takaichi fiscal/growth agenda'. Before scoring, define it as one bounded pre-cutoff fiscal decision or package. If no single sufficiently documented unit can be isolated, replace it rather than bundle unrelated measures.

Status: TIGHTEN — unresolved bounded unit.

## Thailand

### Split combined institutional row
OLD: THA-2024/26-INST-02 — Senate PM-veto expiry / constitutional referendum.

NEW: THA-2026-INST-02 — constitutional referendum authorising the process for a new constitution.

Reason: The old row combined two different events two years apart. The referendum is independently scoreable and directly tests public mandate for institutional redesign.

The May 2024 expiry of the appointed Senate's special PM-selection power remains contextual / historical-transition evidence but is not co-scored in the same row.

Status: READY.

## United States

### Narrow broad institutional family
OLD: USA-2025-INST-01 — spending / rescission / statutory-agency authority conflict.

NEW bounded unit: USA-2025-INST-01 — August 2025 $4.9bn pocket-rescission attempt against congressionally authorised spending.

Reason: The old row combined multiple distinct agency and spending disputes. The pocket-rescission episode directly isolates the constitutional question of executive withholding versus congressional appropriations authority.

Context may include the administration's broader impoundment disputes, but scoring must use the $4.9bn episode as the unit of analysis.

Status: READY.

USAID dismantling may remain a reserve / corroborating institutional case but must not be blended into the same score.
