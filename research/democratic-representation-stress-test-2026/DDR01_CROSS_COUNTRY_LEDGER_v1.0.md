## v1.0 synthesis

Robust:
- no homogeneous immigrant/minority electoral bloc;
- no fixed ideological direction;
- origin/subgroup heterogeneity matters;
- turnout must be separated from preference;
- outcome relevance is not causal decisiveness;
- broad background categories are often too coarse.

Evidence-dependent:
- indirect counter-mobilization is supported but concentrated in historical Denmark/Italy causal studies;
- exact causal magnitude is unresolved;
- several countries remain partial or null because party-choice evidence is missing.
