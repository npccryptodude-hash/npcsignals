Sources: [2023 baseline](BASELINE.md), [2024 baseline](../2024/BASELINE.md), [2025 comparative report](../2025_EUROPE_COMPARATIVE_REPORT.md). EU27 is the shared statistical geography. The number of contributing states with attack rows changes; missing rows do not prove zero. Other and unspecified are harmonised only as a combined analytical category, with original arrest distinctions retained for 2023. The editions use the same three published outcome categories; domestic classification, inclusion decisions and reporting cutoffs can differ. Europol's source is Member State attack and arrest contributions, with court outcomes separately informed by Eurojust and national data. Arrests concern people suspected of terrorism-related offences and do not represent persons involved only in the attacks tabulated.

The 2023 separatist contribution includes 26 events from 2021–22 per TE-SAT 2026 p. 47 n. 44. Thus 120/58/45 is an edition-reported comparison with a material date-scope difference. Mechanical subtraction gives 94 for the 2023 edition excluding those 26 older records; that number is a sensitivity check, not an official revised 2023 total or a corrected national census. The 2024 category overview's five versus six completed other/unspecified events are documented in the existing 2024 baseline. Different victim series and unresolved injury definitions preclude an all-injury year comparison. Three editions support a descriptive comparison only.
