## Conflicts and statistical units

- **Spain:** p. 43 calls the Spanish left-wing/anarchist row foiled; p. 45 describes the 25 January Zaragoza incident as failed. The annex lists one failed and one foiled in Spain alongside the completed jihadist Algeciras event, while p. 54 puts the 9 November Madrid shooting in the failed, unspecified category. Retain Zaragoza as a disputed candidate for the foiled left-wing cell, and preserve both outcome descriptions.
- **Paris:** p. 22 says two killed and one injured on 2 December. The French gendarmerie research note and the later AFP-sourced judicial coverage support one killed; the gendarmerie note records two wounded. The incident row carries the national values. The Europol figures remain separately documented. Incident injuries have not been forced to sum to the aggregate.
- **Duisburg:** p. 21 describes the 9 and 18 April physical events together; the annex has one German completed slot. The joint judicial case is documented, while the underlying national counting decision assigning the statistical unit is not. The allocation represents one identified case example and two physical acts, with no second allocation.
- **Corsica:** p. 10 and p. 61 attribute 70 completed events to the French separatist contribution. The later TE-SAT 2026 p. 47 note 44 specifies that 4 occurred in 2021, 22 in 2022 and 44 in 2023. Keep the published 120 intact as an edition total, with the calendar-year caveat. Individual Corsican events remain unallocated.
