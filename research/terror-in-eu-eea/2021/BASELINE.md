# Calendar-year 2021 baseline — 18 November 2026

The full Europol **TE-SAT 2022**, DOI **10.2813/467703**, covers 1 January–31 December 2021 (pp. 6, 90–91). Europol released it on 13 July 2022 and the Publications Office lists 14 July for its repository. The original Europol PDF was retrieved and inspected on 18 November 2026. It has 96 pages, 2,995,209 bytes and SHA-256 `c8eca87e7d7d65a4097132948eb0ac577cb45328a639fbd160c0b415da5dbc00`. A Malta FIAU copy has different bytes with matching extracted text on the annex pages and selected narrative pages. [Source register](sources.csv).

Annex p. 82: France 5, Germany 3, Sweden 2, Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Hungary and Spain one each. The [attack register](attacks.csv) preserves the original edition and its country/category/outcome cells. Later figures are traced in [the provenance ledger](provenance.csv). Member State returns are based on national law, and qualitative examples may concern extremism, other geographies or other dates (pp. 90–91). The UK contributes no attack or arrest rows to these EU aggregates. P. 22 n. 24 also explains the removal of UK figures from the report's historical 2019 comparison.

Annex p. 83 gives **388 arrests** in 18 Member States: 260 jihadist, 64 right-wing, 19 left-wing/anarchist, 26 separatist, five other and 14 unspecified. The printed Spain row total is **3**. The displayed component cells (39 + 3 + 3 + 2) sum to **47**, which the Council's country table independently displays. Keeping 3 in the operative total would yield 344. The [arrest register](arrests.csv) stores both values, preserves all printed component figures and uses 47 as the reconciled value. Arrests describe suspects and are separate from events and judicial dispositions.

P. 22 gives two victim deaths in France and Spain, excluding the deaths of both perpetrators. Its accounts specify five injured in Roldán and five on the German train, a reconstructed count of at least ten in the report's case descriptions. An independently published all-attack injury aggregate was not verified. Contemporary RTVE reporting records two injured in Roldán and two total deaths including the driver. The Munich court release adjudicates four passenger victims in Germany, a different scope from Europol's reported injury headcount. [Statistics](statistics.csv) preserve source-specific values.

[Coverage](coverage.csv) retains all 31 countries, with missing national counts blank. PSNI's 2021 security counts appear through the 2022 bulletin's prior-calendar-year comparator: two deaths, five bombing incidents, 27 shooting incidents and 130 security-linked arrests under Section 41. Great Britain and Norway have case observations. The police report for Liechtenstein and Icelandic national-security catalogue do not supply a verified comparable attack count for this analysis.
