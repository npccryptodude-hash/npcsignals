Start with the original editions. For 2021 add three foiled right-wing events and reassign four completed separatist events, yielding eight completed, one failed and 13 foiled. Starting from the later 18 would require adding only the four separatist events. The right-wing additions have no effect on Phase 10's 50 for 2022 or 94 for 2023. The 2020 removal is outside the five-year table and appears as context. The relationship between the 26 reassigned entries and other statistical allocations has not been independently deduplicated. The exercise assumes distinct entries with no overlap, applies only documented changes and leaves all original figures fixed.
