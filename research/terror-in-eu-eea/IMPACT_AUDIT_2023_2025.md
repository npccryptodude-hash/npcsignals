# comparative impact audit, 2023–2025

date: 2026-11-18
status: verified EU 2023–2024 primary summaries; 2025 figures cross-checked against published TE-SAT 2026 secondary digest, pending page-level primary annex extraction.

## definitions

attacks_all includes completed, failed and foiled attacks. deaths and injuries refer to victims of completed attacks, not all planned attacks. arrests, convictions and acquittals measure different legal stages and are not measures of successful attacks. geographical scope here is EU only, not the project's fixed 31-country study area.

## source-verified comparisons

2023: 120 attacks; jihadist 14, left-wing/anarchist 32, separatist 70, right-wing 2; jihadist deaths 6, injuries 12; jihadist completed attacks 5.
2024: 58 attacks; jihadist 24, left-wing/anarchist 21, separatist 4, right-wing 1, other/unspecified 8; jihadist deaths 5, injuries 18; jihadist completed attacks 6.
2025: 45 attacks, 22 completed, 3 failed, 20 foiled; jihadist 24 (9 completed), left-wing/anarchist 12, right-wing 5; jihadist deaths 5, injuries 81; one further death attributed to right-wing attack. The 2025 figures were reviewed via a contemporary eucrim digest and should be checked against the original Europol report pages before marking independently extracted.

## analytical cautions

- In 2023 the highest attack count was separatist; in 2024 and 2025 jihadist attacks were the largest single category.
- Jihadist attacks were the most lethal in all three years according to Europol.
- The 2025 injury count is materially higher than 2024 and merits incident-level source reconciliation.
- A change in reported attack counts cannot be equated with a change in underlying threat without accounting for geography, classification, reporting, and completed/foiled status.
- This comparison does not establish a 31-country death toll. UK, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein are not included in the EU totals.
