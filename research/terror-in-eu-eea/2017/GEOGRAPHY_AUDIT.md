# UK and Northern Ireland in the 2017 tables — 18 November 2026

The UK was an EU member both in the reference year and at June 2018 publication. The annex tables retain it. Fixed EU27 follows today's geographical membership, using identified UK rows as the subtraction. See the [source register](sources.csv) for the two Europol editions and UK records.

| Measure | Original EU28 | Included UK contribution | Fixed EU27 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Attack and security entries | 205 | 107 | 98 |
| Arrests | 1,219 | 412 | 807 |
| Unspecified arrests | 428 | 412 | 16 |
| Jihadist deaths, same reported scope | 62 | 35 | 27 |

The results also match independent sums over the non-UK rows of the respective annexes. The 107 comprise 14 jihadist, five right-wing and 88 Northern Ireland security-related entries. Page 9 nn. 1 and 4 and chapter p. 45 split those 88 into 58 shootings and 30 bombings; five targeted national security. The five are a subset. They are not subtracted again or added as five separate events. The 412 UK arrests exclude 173 Northern Ireland arrests before publication. The latter are recorded separately. The [statistics register](statistics.csv) also preserves the UK parliamentary national-security series and distinguishes narrower measurement and PSNI periods from the Europol return. UK Independent Reviewer chapter 3 independently confirms the 107 and 88 counts.

TE-SAT 2019 carries forward 205 and 1,219 for 2017 in its UK-inclusive historical charts. No later full fixed-EU27 2017 comparator was verified in this pass. The derived 98 and 807 therefore use TE-SAT 2018 country-table arithmetic. Subtractions are by jurisdiction and do not amend any individual classification.
