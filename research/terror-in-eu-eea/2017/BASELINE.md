The attacks annex (pp. 54–55) gives nine contributing states: UK 107, France 54, Spain 16, Italy 14, Greece eight, Belgium and Germany two each, Finland and Sweden one each. The arrest annex (pp. 55–56) gives **1,219 arrests** in 19 states: 705 jihadist, 36 left-wing/anarchist, 30 separatist, 20 right-wing and 428 unspecified. The UK contributed 412 unspecified arrests. Reporting also includes qualitative examples outside tabulated scope; Eurojust's court series is a separate measure.

## Outcome evidence

The jihadist chapter p. 23 explicitly gives **10 completed, 12 failed and 11 foiled**. Its ten fatal cases match the reported number of completed events, including distinct Barcelona and Cambrils allocations. The annex describes five UK jihadist attacks and nine disrupted plots, terminology that does not explicitly allocate the nine to the three outcome labels. It also gives two completed out of 11 French attacks. The [country-cell dataset](attacks.csv) uses only supported joins, including identifiable failed and left-wing cases. Its **14 completed, three failed, six foiled and 182 unresolved** are allocations across country-category rows of all types, with a separate aggregate outcome margin for jihadism. The two sets of counts describe different levels and should not be added. Spain's left-wing chapter describes two attacks carried out and five foiled. The 60% left-wing completion wording in annex 1 is approximate, with no exact all-country integer table. The whole-edition outcome split remains unverified.

## Victims and coverage

The original headcount is **68 killed and 844 injured**, excluding casualties of Northern Ireland security incidents. Europol attributes **62 deaths and 819 injuries** to jihadist attacks. The chapter's 35 UK jihadist deaths are sourced from open reports and reconcile with Westminster, Manchester and London Bridge. The five other country subtotals total **27 fixed-EU27 jihadist deaths** (Spain 16, Sweden five, France three, Finland two and Germany one). There is no full scope-compatible fixed-EU27 injury or all-ideology fatality split in the sources checked. Spain's national report gives a broader injury figure than the original Europol event narratives; preserve both sources with their definitions. Case-specific counts were not used to force a reconciliation.

The [31-country register](coverage.csv) records UK inclusion in the historical EU28 aggregate and exclusion from fixed EU27. Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein are outside both aggregates, with comparable national counts unverified. Missing country rows remain blank. The [crosswalk](crosswalk.csv) has 27 adequate identifications, one candidate and 177 unidentified slots.
