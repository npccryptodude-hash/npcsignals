# EU terrorism statistics, 2020–2025 — 18 November 2026

## Original annual editions

| Reference year | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Original attacks | 57 | 15 | 28 | 120 | 58 | 45 |
| Arrested persons | 449 | 388 | 380 | 426 | 449 | 486 |

[2020 baseline](BASELINE.md) and [2021–2025 comparison](../2021/COMPARABILITY_2021_2025.md). EU27 comparable geography begins here with a reporting qualification. The UK departed on 31 January 2020 and the transition ended on 31 December. TE-SAT 2021 already separates UK 2020 figures. Its 2019 chart shows 119 including 64 UK incidents, with 55 shown separately for the EU without the UK. Subsequent editions need jurisdiction-specific coverage for Great Britain, Northern Ireland and the three non-EU EEA countries. UK security counts cannot be substituted for EU measures. Event classifications, timing, victim definitions and arrest units need separate consideration. No trend inference is made from this short and partly retrospective series.

## Later official historical figures

TE-SAT 2023 first shows **56 for 2020** and **18 for 2021**, with footnote explanations. Its figures are observations of a later publication vintage. TE-SAT 2024 includes 26 retrospective French events in its published 2023 cohort. TE-SAT 2026 clarifies the event-year split. No official revised occurrence-year total of 22 for 2021, 50 for 2022 or 94 for 2023 has been verified.

One German record is removed. Three late French right-wing cases are added to 2021, along with four older separatist cases reassigned from the 2023 cohort. Twenty-two more are reassigned to 2022. The exercise assumes the late cohorts are distinct and applies only the documented changes. It supplies no event-level deduplication, back-casting of victim figures or replacement official series.
