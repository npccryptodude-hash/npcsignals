# Calendar-year 2020 baseline — 18 November 2026

Europol published TE-SAT 2021 on 22 June 2021. Its reference year is 2020. The [source register](sources.csv) identifies the original full PDF, retrieved directly on 18 November 2026, and the FIAU mirror. The original is 113 pages, 11,189,122 bytes, DOI 10.2813/677724, SHA-256 `ac5011b7733de4df27fba02acf3b578704899aaa7f9eba7136ddd1e5133defb5`. The mirror has different bytes. This joins the published annex to the jihadist (pp. 13, 43–48), right-wing (pp. 18–19) and left-wing narratives (pp. 21, 92–93). The detailed left-wing chapter labels all 24 Italian events completed and the French plot foiled. The overview separately calls an Italian parcel incident failed and pp. 92–93 describe a parcel that did not explode. It is unclear whether that incident is inside the 24 and what defines completion in that cell. A sensitivity treating one of the 24 as failed would give 34 completed and two failed among the allocated records. The separatist narrative says the attacks were carried out and gives thematic detail, with no explicit complete/failed/foiled breakdown by country. If all 14 are treated as completed, the broad reconstructions would be 49/1/7 using the chapter labels, or 48/2/7 with the Italian sensitivity. Neither reconstruction replaces the original 57. The CSV preserves unverified separatist outcomes as unresolved.

Six Member States have attack rows: Italy 24, France 15, Spain nine, Germany six, Belgium two and Austria one. The annex records 449 persons arrested across 17 states: 254 jihadism, 34 right-wing, 52 left-wing/anarchist, 39 separatist, one other and 69 unspecified. The Belgium footnote qualifies its 58 unclassified arrests: most were linked to jihadism, but Belgium could not allocate them by ideology. The annex classification is retained. Methodology pp. 107–109 explains national-law inputs, cross-checking and validation by Member States and the specific meaning of judicially warranted arrests. Qualitative cases can have different coverage and arrest totals are not a proxy for attacks.

The overview (p. 12) gives **21 victim deaths and at least 54 injured**, with 12 deaths from jihadist and nine from right-wing attacks. Europol pp. 13 and 43 say **more than 47** injured in jihadist attacks. The Parliament summary gives 47 and presents 54 overall without the original lower-bound qualifier. These source-specific differences do not warrant a revised victim series. The Vienna case counts 17 directly injured and ten injured during escape in Europol's narrative. No victim adjustment is imputed from the later removal of one attack record.

[Country coverage](coverage.csv) lists 31 states. TE-SAT keeps the UK outside the 57 EU attacks and 449 arrests and separately records 62 UK incidents and 185 arrests. The 62 comprises six terrorist attacks/plots and 56 Northern Ireland security-related incidents (p. 12 n. 2). The latter aggregates 39 shootings and 17 bombings as reported in the TE-SAT 2021 vintage (p. 23). Store this source-defined composite separately from PSNI bulletins and financial-year counts. Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein have catalogue-level coverage with comparable 2020 figures unverified.
