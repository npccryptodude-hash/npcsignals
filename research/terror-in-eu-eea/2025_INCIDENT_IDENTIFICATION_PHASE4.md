Narni is in this tier because the failed classification remains inferred.

## Corrections and source limits

The assignment's two Belgian jihadist and four French foiled right-wing counts are corrected against p. 19. Belgium reported one foiled jihadist and one foiled other/unspecified event. France reported one completed and three foiled right-wing events. Ireland accounts for the fourth foiled right-wing event EU-wide. The earlier comparative tables already use these counts.

Italy's January travel-agency event is individually identifiable by the unique dated Europol case example and completed damage, although locality and the national case file are still unknown. The Italian Prosecutor General independently identifies Narni and the Unareti fire. The latter source dates Unareti to 26–27 February; local reporting dates the apparently corresponding fire to 27–28 February and reports initial uncertainty about whether it was deliberate. A March Rome Tesla fire is a separate candidate supported by RAI reporting of the investigation and claim. No claim is treated as judicial identification or direction. The Roman police custody announcement identifies two additional domestic fires, Castel Gandolfo and Albano Laziale. The early Albano anarchist lead and reported smoke injuries do not support allocating either case to the Italian TE-SAT cell.

The Thessaloniki bank explosion of 3 May was checked against early police-attributed reporting and coverage of the 2026 proceedings. Those sources do not establish an other/unspecified terrorist designation or a submission link. It remains outside the candidate allocation. The Maltese court results reviewed did not supply a matched terrorist event. Belgium's OCAM publishes threat reports, while the federal prosecutor confirms judicial proceedings for Antwerp. Neither supplies the second TE-SAT event identity.
