## incident

on 18 may 2025 four people were critically injured in a knife attack outside a venue in bielefeld. the german federal prosecutor took over the investigation on 20 may 2025 citing suspected religious motivation.

primary prosecutor statement:
https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/6038652

## prosecution

on 3 december 2025 the federal prosecutor announced an indictment dated 26 november 2025 alleging membership in the islamic state (is), four counts of attempted murder and dangerous bodily harm. the indictment alleged the accused had joined is in syria in 2015 and performed fighting, guard, border and administrative duties through november 2016, and had remained ideologically attached after entering germany in 2023. allegations are not themselves convictions.

primary prosecutor statement:
https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/6171449

## court outcome

on 1 june 2026 the oberlandesgericht düsseldorf sentenced the accused to life imprisonment with preventive detention, according to german public broadcaster wdr's contemporaneous court report. the report says the court found four attempted murders and the particular gravity of guilt. defence announced intent to appeal. verify original court press release and final appellate status before marking final.

secondary contemporaneous court report:
https://www1.wdr.de/nrw/bielefeld/lebenslange-haft-messerangriff-bielefeld-100.html

## relationship and evidence safeguards

the federal prosecutor alleged prior is membership and organisational activity in syria. this is different from evidence of direct is operational instructions for the 2025 bielefeld attack. do not conflate historical membership with command of this particular act. record the judicial finding from the full judgment when available.

## appellate and relationship update — 2026-11-18

A defence lawyer filed a revision with the Bundesgerichtshof on 8 June 2026, following the Düsseldorf court's life sentence and finding of particularly serious guilt. A later appellate disposition has not been established. https://www1.wdr.de/nrw/bielefeld/verurteilter-bielefeld-cutie-attentaeter-legt-revision-ein-100.html

The federal prosecutor's original indictment alleges IS membership beginning in Syria in 2015, combat and organisational functions until November 2016, and continued ideological allegiance after entry to Germany in 2023. https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/6171449

WDR reports the life sentence, four attempted murders, and the court finding of particularly serious guilt. The original judgment has not been verified here. The sources reviewed do not independently establish that IS commanded the 18 May 2025 attack. Prior membership and operational tasking are separate facts.

## Original Europol — 2026-11-18

[TE-SAT 2026 p. 27](https://www.europol.europa.eu/cms/sites/default/files/documents/EU-TE-SAT-2026.pdf#page=27) explicitly names Bielefeld and four critically injured victims. It describes past IS membership and a continuing connection after arrival in Germany. The public passage does not specify the basis or nature of that ongoing connection. The indictment offers evidence of alleged membership and ideological allegiance. Neither public record establishes tasking for the incident. No subsequent appeal resolution was located in this pass.

## Arrest and casualty observations — reviewed 2026-11-18

The [joint police and prosecutor update of 20 May](https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/12522/6038611) confirms arrest on the evening of 19 May and communication of the detention warrant on 20 May. The [12:39 update](https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/12522/6036567) and [17:48 update](https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/12522/6036625) on 18 May each gave at least five injured. The serious-injury subtotal changed from three to four, with one described as in life-threatening condition at 17:48. This qualifies the earlier headline of four injured. Europol p. 27 counts four critically injured and the prosecution charged four attempted murders. The fifth person's status in the Europol aggregate remains unknown.
