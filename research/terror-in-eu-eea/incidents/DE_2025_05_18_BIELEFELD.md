## incident

on 18 may 2025 four people were critically injured in a knife attack outside a venue in bielefeld. the german federal prosecutor took over the investigation on 20 may 2025 citing suspected religious motivation.

primary prosecutor statement:
https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/6038652

## prosecution

on 3 december 2025 the federal prosecutor announced an indictment dated 26 november 2025 alleging membership in the islamic state (is), four counts of attempted murder and dangerous bodily harm. the indictment alleged the accused had joined is in syria in 2015 and performed fighting, guard, border and administrative duties through november 2016, and had remained ideologically attached after entering germany in 2023. allegations are not themselves convictions.

https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/6171449

## court outcome

on 1 june 2026 the oberlandesgericht düsseldorf sentenced the accused to life imprisonment with preventive detention, according to german public broadcaster wdr's contemporaneous court report. the report says the court found four attempted murders and the particular gravity of guilt. defence announced intent to appeal. verify original court press release and final appellate status before marking final.
