## event

on 13 february 2025 a driver struck participants in a ver.di demonstration in munich near seidlstrasse. two people died. the suspect was arrested at the scene.

## critical judicial update — 2026-11-18

**Earlier text describing the trial as ongoing is superseded.** On 6 August 2026, the Oberlandesgericht München, case 7 St 3/25, convicted Farhad N. of two murders, 23 attempted murders, 19 counts of dangerous bodily harm, three counts of intentional bodily harm and dangerous interference with road traffic. It imposed life imprisonment and found particularly serious guilt. Court source: https://www.bayern.verfassungsgerichtshof.de/gerichte-und-behoerden/oberlandesgerichte/muenchen/presse/2026/53.php

The court found deliberate high-speed driving into the ver.di demonstration and a political-religious motivation; the judgment found the defendant fully criminally responsible. The court release counts **43 injured persons**, a critical primary-source victim-count datum. City of Munich's victim memorial page gives **44 injured**: https://www.muenchen.de/aktuell/anschlag-demo-februar-2025. The police security report says **more than 50**; a Europol-based secondary account says **57**. These are distinct counts in different publications and should not be merged without original definitions and as-of dates. The court release's 43 is not automatically the same reporting universe as the 81 EU aggregate.

The police investigation found no evidence of participation by additional persons or groups. The court release establishes religious-political motivation but does **not** establish operational direction by a named terrorist organisation. Do not infer IS command.

The court ultimately found 23 attempted murders. Indictment and judgment offence counts measure legal findings or allegations and belong outside the injury tally unless a source separately gives a victim count.
