## incident and investigation
On 22 February 2025 a man attacked people near the Canal Couvert market in Mulhouse. One person died; several municipal officials were injured. The French national anti-terror prosecutor (PNAT) took jurisdiction.
Contemporaneous reporting: https://www.estrepublicain.fr/faits-divers-justice/2025/02/22/attaque-mortelle-au-couteau-le-parquet-antiterroriste-saisi

## procedural status
Le Monde reported that on 26 February 2025 the suspect was formally placed under investigation for assassination and attempted assassination of public officials in relation to a terrorist enterprise, among other counts:
https://www.lemonde.fr/societe/article/2025/02/27/le-parcours-de-brahim-a-l-assaillant-presume-du-marche-de-mulhouse_6566297_3224.html

A 22 February 2026 retrospective reports that the suspect remained in pretrial detention and the investigation continued. It quotes PNAT saying the suspect partially acknowledged the acts in custody but denied adhering to jihadist ideology.
https://www.dna.fr/faits-divers-justice/2026/02/22/radicalise-ou-desequilibre-qui-est-l-auteur-de-l-attaque-au-couteau
