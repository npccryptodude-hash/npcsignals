# Lyon — 10 September 2025

Review date 2026-11-18. CRGN note 125 records a fatal attack and PNAT includes Lyon in its national 2025 jihadist-inspired count. Le Monde reports the principal suspect was arrested in Italy on 2 October. It quotes a 13 October joint prosecutor statement expanding the judicial investigation to assassination linked to a terrorist enterprise and terrorist criminal association. These are investigative classifications. No judgment, finality, independently verified group link or case-specific financial support was established. Europol membership remains unresolved.
