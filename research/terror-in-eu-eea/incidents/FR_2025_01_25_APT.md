# Apt — 25 January 2025

Review date 2026-11-18. France's Interior Ministry confirms the investigation and joint assignment to the DGSI and judicial police. CRGN note 125, p. 1, records two injured (customer and security guard) and no death in its account. PNAT includes Apt in its national 2025 jihadist-inspired attack count. Le Dauphiné reports that a judicial investigation was opened for attempted assassinations linked to a terrorist enterprise and that the suspect was placed under formal investigation and detained on 29 January 2025. Later disposition, trial and any appeal remain unverified. Review of public sources did not establish organisational membership, direct contact, command or case-specific financing. Europol membership remains unresolved.
