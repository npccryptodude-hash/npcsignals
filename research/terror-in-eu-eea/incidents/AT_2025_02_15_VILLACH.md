# villach, austria — 2025-02-15

reviewed 2026-11-18
status: completed jihadist attack; **conviction confirmed by court announcement; later finality reported by ORF citing the court spokesperson**
fatalities: 1 (14-year-old boy)
injuries: 5 (serious)
weapon: knife

## official prosecution
On 15 December 2025 the Klagenfurt prosecutor indicted a 23-year-old man for terrorist offences including murder and attempted murder, terrorist association and criminal organisation. The prosecutor reported that the investigation found **no evidence of accomplices**. This is an investigative finding, not proof that no ideological contacts existed.
Primary source: https://www.justiz.gv.at/file/2c92fd159b026e10019b21656be83ccf.de.0/2025_12_15_pressemitteilung_sta%20%20klagenfurt.pdf

## trial and finality
On 27 May 2026 the Klagenfurt regional court jury unanimously found the defendant guilty of terrorist offences, murder and attempted murder, terrorist association and criminal organisation. The court imposed life imprisonment. The defendant waived appeal; at announcement the prosecution had not yet declared its decision.
Primary court release: https://www.justiz.gv.at/lg-klagenfurt/landesgericht-klagenfurt/medienstelle/pressemitteilungen/attentat-in-villach-angeklagter-zu-lebenslanger-haftstrafe-verurteilt.12d2.de.html

On 2 June 2026 ORF reported, citing court spokesman Markus Kitz, that the prosecution had not appealed within the deadline; the judgment was final.
https://kaernten.orf.at/stories/3356833/

## organisation relationship
Court conviction includes terrorist association / criminal organisation and records intent to intimidate civilians not following IS goals. The prosecution's absence-of-accomplices finding does not establish an absence of ideological allegiance or association; nor does the conviction alone prove direct command by an identified IS handler for this attack. Keep association, allegiance, operational direction, and accomplices as separate evidence fields.
