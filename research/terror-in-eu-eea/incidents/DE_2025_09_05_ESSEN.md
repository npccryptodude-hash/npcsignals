## judicial update — 2026-11-18 review

**The prior statement 'conviction unknown' is superseded.** On 22 September 2026 the Oberlandesgericht Düsseldorf, case III-5 St 2/26, sentenced the then-18-year-old to **nine years of juvenile detention**. The court found two attempted murders (teacher and homeless man) with dangerous bodily injury, plus other offences. The earlier alleged attempted murder of a caretaker was not upheld as attempted murder. The judgment was **not final at announcement**. The court ordered placement in a social-therapeutic institution and reserved possible preventive detention.
