## France and casualty arithmetic

[CRGN note 125](https://www.gendarmerie.interieur.gouv.fr/crgn/content/download/57190/file/Research_Note_CRGN_125_Terrorism_radical_Movements_2025.pdf) p. 1 records 2 dead and 9 injured across three national cases, including 7 injured in Mulhouse and 2 in Apt. The fatality tally would fit if both fatal French events were included (Munich 2, Villach 1, Mulhouse 1 and Lyon 1). This is only an arithmetic fit. It depends on the same victim universe, no other contributing deaths and comparable observation dates. PNAT reports seven thwarted attacks in its jihadist segment, while Europol p. 19 reports eight foiled French jihadist attacks. Both completed and foiled discrepancies remain open, alongside the possibility of distinct reporting definitions or timing.

Munich 57 and Bielefeld 4 are explicit TE-SAT incident counts. Villach is explicitly described with one death and several injured. Thus 61 injuries are numerically specified by Europol in the two named German examples, leaving 20 of the EU 81 to account for before adding any other counts. National values may have a different observation basis. No full nine-row victim ledger is claimed. The source-specific injury figures in the CSV package must never be added across alternative counts of the same event. Munich's official series confirms 57/81 (70.37%) using Europol values.
