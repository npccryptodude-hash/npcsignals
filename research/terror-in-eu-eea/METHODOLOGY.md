# method and definitions

## scope

include all 27 current eu members and norway, iceland, liechtenstein. distinguish geographic territory at event date from institutional membership at reporting date. track the uk separately when historical europol editions include it; never silently combine it with the fixed 30-country series.

## categories

use the original source classification and version. analytical categories: jihadist; right-wing; left-wing/anarchist; ethno-nationalist/separatist; other/single-issue; unclear/unclassified. an ideology classification describes a documented motive or source label, not a religious or political population.

## measurement

separate completed, failed and foiled attacks. separately track deaths, injuries, arrests, convictions and acquittals. arrests are not convictions; a foiled plot is not a completed attack. percentages must show denominator, territory, year, reporting completeness and classification source. never convert historic frequency into a probability forecast.

## evidence

confirmed = primary documentation supports the precise claim; reported = attributed secondary reporting without independent primary confirmation; unknown = insufficient evidence. a judicial allegation must not be recast as a proven fact. record source quotation/page, access date, document version, and uncertainty. use source-linked relation records rather than inferred guilt by association.

## safety and editorial integrity

do not publish personal identifiers of uninvolved individuals, addresses, operational attack instructions, or actionable terrorist financing methods. distinguish organisational membership, logistical support, communication, ideological inspiration and unverified association. avoid presenting islam or muslim communities as equivalent to jihadist extremism. document contradictory evidence and corrections.

## publication gate

independent source and arithmetic checks, cross-border deduplication, country coverage audit, review of legal classification, privacy and harm review, explicit unresolved questions. github evidence first; medium synthesis only after review.
