# Revolutionary Class Self-Defense (RCS)

## Emergence, aims and structure

The US National Counterterrorism Center's [June 2026 group profile](https://www.odni.gov/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/CT-Guide-Revolutionary-Class-Self-Defense_508.pdf) describes RCS as an anonymous Greek anarchist violent-extremist group formed in **early 2024**. NCTC attributes to it anti-capitalist and anti-authoritarian objectives, including challenging the legitimacy of Greek state and economic institutions through political violence.

NCTC assesses that the group operates principally in the Athens area, probably has **fewer than 40 members** in small cells, and changes names or divides into smaller cells to avoid detection. These are **US intelligence estimates**, not independently verified membership, an authenticated organisational roster or judicial findings.

## Reported methods and target selection

NCTC describes the use of improvised explosive devices and parcel bombs against governmental and economic infrastructure. It reports that warning calls are typically used to reduce harm to bystanders. That description is an agency pattern assessment, not a guarantee of safety, an assessment of legal culpability or a statement about every incident.

NCTC attributes to the group political rationales involving economic policy, industrial accidents and solidarity with Palestinian nationalism. These are **reported justifications** and must not be treated as proof of actual perpetrator motive in a particular case without event-specific corroboration.

The 2025 statistical match is adequate in Part 1; the 2024 case remains a candidate. No reviewed Greek judgment establishes named participants or group operational command.

## US designation

The June 2026 NCTC profile states that the US Department of State designated RCS in **November 2025** as both an SDGT and an FTO, in a designation described as associated with the Antifa movement. This is a **secondary official agency description of a designation**, not an independently reopened State Department designation notice or Federal Register entry. The precise effective date, legal instruments, aliases and status should be checked against those primary records before publication. The designation does not itself resolve individual responsibility for either Greek incident.

## Unresolved questions

3. Authenticate any original responsibility statements and distinguish their content from Europol's report of them.
4. Test the small-cell and name-change assessments against independently dated national sources.
5. Keep the 2024 candidate statistical match separate from the adequate 2025 match.
