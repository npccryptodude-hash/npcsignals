# Islamic State (IS): organisation, claims and case-level evidence

## Identity, historical scope and aims

Europol classifies Islamic State (IS) as a jihadist terrorist organisation. IS is distinct from the broader jihadist ideological category and from its affiliates, self-declared adherents and persons inspired by its propaganda. The organisation's history includes territorial control in Iraq and Syria, but the reviewed European case registers are **not** a complete source for a dated history of its leadership, finances, territorial governance or changing strategic objectives. Those questions require separate original primary records before an organisation-wide historical conclusion is published.

For the present research, the source-supported organisational objective can only be described at the **Europol classification level**: IS is a jihadist terrorist organisation whose propaganda and affiliated networks feature in European investigations. This does not establish that every jihadist attacker sought the same political outcome or acted for IS.

## Detailed legal anchor: Solingen

The German Federal Court of Justice (BGH) reported that the revision was rejected on **12 May 2026** and the underlying conviction became final. Its [22 May 2026 announcement](https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/2026091.html) summarises court findings including IS membership and advice or encouragement from contacts. Europol separately records an IS responsibility claim and messaging. A **final membership finding** and a **reported responsibility claim** are distinct propositions; neither automatically establishes who selected the target, provided resources or exercised operational control. Those additional links require case-specific proof.

## Analytical distinctions

- **Ideology:** a person's beliefs or expressions do not themselves establish organisational affiliation.
- **Propaganda exposure:** content may be influential without an authenticated contact or instruction.
- **Allegiance:** a declaration by an individual is not proof of acceptance as a member.
- **Contact:** communication can be documented without showing financing, assistance or command.
- **Membership:** where judicially established, the precise scope and dates of the finding matter.
- **Assistance or financing:** distinguish aid to a person or network from funding or direction of a particular attack.
- **Claim of responsibility:** a reported IS claim is a claim; source authenticity and factual attribution are separate.
- **Operational command:** requires evidence linking an instruction or decision to the particular incident.

## Primary German judicial records: organisational history, support and financing (20 November 2026)

### BGH AK 100–106/23, 24 January 2024 — pretrial detention review

The [BGH original order, ECLI:DE:BGH:2024:240124BAK100.23.0](https://juris.bundesgerichtshof.de/cgi-bin/rechtsprechung/document.py?Art=en&Blank=1.pdf&Datum=2024&Gericht=bgh&Sort=1026&anz=3646&nr=136396&pos=4), concerns seven suspects in a German alleged domestic group and alleged support to IS/ISPK. **Procedural stage:** continuation of pretrial detention under sections 121–122 StPO, on the basis of strong suspicion; **not a conviction**. The order says the suspected group sought attacks in Germany or western Europe and allegedly collected and transferred funds in support of IS. The court records alleged communications with persons abroad connected to ISPK, while expressly stating that the contents of those communications had **not** been established. It also records that concrete attack plans and immediate preparations had not been developed before the July 2023 arrests (order, paras. 14–15). This is not proof that IS central leadership ordered a specific European attack.

The order's legal discussion treats IS as a foreign terrorist organisation and describes ISPK as a subordinate IS organisation for the purposes of that assessment (paras. 43 onward). Preserve the source's legal and temporal context rather than generalising this classification to every self-declared affiliate.

### BGH StB 50/25, 30 October 2025 — alleged support by a non-member

The [BGH original order, ECLI:DE:BGH:2025:301025BSTB50.25.0](https://juris.bundesgerichtshof.de/cgi-bin/rechtsprechung/document.py?Art=en&Blank=1.pdf&Datum=Aktuell&Gericht=bgh&Sort=12288&anz=1072&nr=143630&pos=4), rejected an appeal against a detention warrant. **Procedural stage:** detention review on strong suspicion, **not a merits conviction**. The court describes allegations that a woman collected and transferred funds for imprisoned IS members and their families and relayed correspondence intended to sustain support for IS. The order explicitly describes her as **not herself an IS member** (paras. 10–11). This supports the legal distinction between suspected assistance and membership, not a finding of guilt.

The order also provides a judicially stated **historical account of IS's aims** (paras. 12–19): establishment of a state governed by its ideology and interpretation of sharia in Iraq and the wider Levant; renaming to Islamic State upon the June 2014 proclamation of a caliphate; loss of territorial control by March 2019; and continued clandestine activity and international affiliates. This is an original **German court's account** of the organisation's history, not an authenticated original IS manifesto. Time-sensitive force estimates in the order must not be represented as current 2026 figures.

### Research implications

These two judicial records distinguish **suspected domestic cell organisation**, **reported contact with ISPK**, **suspected financing or support**, **membership**, and **operational direction**. They do not create additional confirmed attacks in the annual Europol denominator. Before attributing any of the alleged conduct as proven, locate subsequent indictments, trial judgments and appellate outcomes, if any.


## Follow-up disposition search: AK 100–106/23 and StB 50/25 (20 November 2026)

**AK 100–106/23 — later indictment confirmed, final verdict not verified.** The German Federal Prosecutor's Office announced on 24 April 2024 that seven defendants were indicted at the Düsseldorf Higher Regional Court on **28 March 2024** for forming and participating in an alleged domestic terrorist organisation. Six of the seven (excluding Mukhammadshujo A.) were additionally accused of supporting IS. The prosecutor alleged that several thousand euros were transferred to IS representatives abroad. These are **charges**, not established trial facts. [Original federal prosecutor statement republished verbatim by Presseportal](https://www.presseportal.de/blaulicht/pm/14981/5764903). [ARD/SWR trial reporting, 28 July 2024](https://www.tagesschau.de/investigativ/swr/is-khorasan-terrorismus-prozess-deutschland-tuerkei-100.html) records the Düsseldorf trial opening. The searched materials did not establish a later judgment or appellate finality. **Status: indicted and tried; final disposition unverified.**

**StB 50/25 — later verdict not verified.** The original 30 October 2025 BGH detention-review order remains the latest case-specific judicial document verified in this search. Its alleged transfers, recruitment and correspondence are not convictions. **Status: pretrial as last verified; subsequent disposition unknown.**

**False-match safeguard:** The widely indexed Düsseldorf IS verdict dated **31 May 2022** concerns **five defendants**, and predates the July 2023 arrests of the seven persons in AK 100–106/23. It is a **different case** and must not be attached to this record. [ARD 2022 report](https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/is-terroristen-haft-urteil-duesseldorf-101.html).

Do not convert a failed verdict search into a claim that no verdict exists. Future retrieval should use docket **AK 100–106/23**, prosecutor's **28 March 2024 indictment**, defendant names and the Düsseldorf court's assigned trial docket.
