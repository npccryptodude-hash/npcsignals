# Islamic State (IS): organisation, claims and case-level evidence

## Identity, historical scope and aims

Europol classifies Islamic State (IS) as a jihadist terrorist organisation. IS is distinct from the broader jihadist ideological category and from its affiliates, self-declared adherents and persons inspired by its propaganda. The organisation's history includes territorial control in Iraq and Syria, but the reviewed European case registers are **not** a complete source for a dated history of its leadership, finances, territorial governance or changing strategic objectives. Those questions require separate original primary records before an organisation-wide historical conclusion is published.

For the present research, the source-supported organisational objective can only be described at the **Europol classification level**: IS is a jihadist terrorist organisation whose propaganda and affiliated networks feature in European investigations. This does not establish that every jihadist attacker sought the same political outcome or acted for IS.

## Detailed legal anchor: Solingen

The German Federal Court of Justice (BGH) reported that the revision was rejected on **12 May 2026** and the underlying conviction became final. Its [22 May 2026 announcement](https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/2026091.html) summarises court findings including IS membership and advice or encouragement from contacts. Europol separately records an IS responsibility claim and messaging. A **final membership finding** and a **reported responsibility claim** are distinct propositions; neither automatically establishes who selected the target, provided resources or exercised operational control. Those additional links require case-specific proof.

## Analytical distinctions

- **Ideology:** a person's beliefs or expressions do not themselves establish organisational affiliation.
- **Propaganda exposure:** content may be influential without an authenticated contact or instruction.
- **Allegiance:** a declaration by an individual is not proof of acceptance as a member.
- **Contact:** communication can be documented without showing financing, assistance or command.
- **Membership:** where judicially established, the precise scope and dates of the finding matter.
- **Assistance or financing:** distinguish aid to a person or network from funding or direction of a particular attack.
- **Claim of responsibility:** a reported IS claim is a claim; source authenticity and factual attribution are separate.
- **Operational command:** requires evidence linking an instruction or decision to the particular incident.
