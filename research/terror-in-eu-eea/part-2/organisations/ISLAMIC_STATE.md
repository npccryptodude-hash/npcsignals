# islamic state

Europol treats Islamic State (IS) as a jihadist terrorist organisation. In its 2026 edition, it distinguishes group propaganda and inspiration from cases with evidence of interpersonal contact. The report discusses the group and its affiliates separately from the people associated with individual entries.

In the 2024 Solingen case, Germany's Federal Court of Justice confirmed dismissal of the revision on 12 May 2026, making the underlying judgment final. The court's 22 May notice summarises findings including participation as a member and advice and encouragement from IS contacts. Europol separately reports messaging and a responsibility claim. The reviewed documents establish those scoped propositions. They do not establish financing or operational command in this case, and the case cannot supply a Europe-wide rate of involvement.
