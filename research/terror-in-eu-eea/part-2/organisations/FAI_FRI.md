# informal anarchist federation / international revolutionary front (FAI/FRI)

## Classification and organisational limits

Europol's [TE-SAT 2017 left-wing and anarchist chapter](https://www.europol.europa.eu/tesat/2017/4.LEFT-WING_AND_ANARCHIST_TERRORISM.html) identifies the Italian FAI/FRI label and describes several 2016 incidents attributed to named cells. Europol's [TE-SAT 2023 category definition](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1694f1b5-9a38-11ee-b164-01aa75ed71a1.0001.01/DOC_1) also cites FAI/FRI as an example of anarchist terrorism. These are official analytical classifications, not an independently established hierarchy or membership register.

The shared label does not prove common operational command. Attribution must be assessed incident by incident.

## Documented and attributed 2016 activity

Europol's TE-SAT 2017 reports 16 left-wing/anarchist terrorist attacks in Italy in 2016. Within its narrative, it describes:

- January: an incendiary device outside the Civitavecchia courthouse in Rome, attributed to a named FAI/FRI cell, with property damage.
- June: two parcel-bomb incidents, one intercepted at the European Food Safety Authority in **Parma on 7 June 2016**, the other exploding at a biotechnology company in **Milan on 9 June 2016**, with a minor injury. Europol reports an FAI/FRI-cell attribution. **The original Italian intelligence report says neither parcel attack was directly claimed**, while assessing a probable common campaign associated with a *Nucleo Danaus plexippus* FAI/FRI communication. The agency assessment is not an authenticated claim by either perpetrator or a judicial finding.
- Bologna: an explosive device outside a Carabinieri station, which Europol describes as **believed** to have been placed by an FAI/FRI cell.

These descriptions support **source-attributed assessments and reported claims**, with different levels of confidence even within the same paragraph. In particular, the Italian government's unclaimed-parcel distinction must be preserved alongside Europol's broader attribution. They do not, on their own, establish judicial findings, membership or common command. The 16 Italian statistical entries must not all be attributed to FAI/FRI: Europol discusses other actors and themes in the same chapter.

Europol describes attacks on migration-related facilities and associated companies, criminal-justice institutions and other targets in its broader Italian 2016 account. These targets show a range of grievances and target choices, not a single verified organisation-wide motive.

## Later cited claim: Rome 2022

For 2022, Europol reports a Rome responsibility claim under the Augusto Masetti Brigade / FAI-FRI label. This is a **reported claim**. Part 1 records an adequate match for the foiled entry and keeps the claimant relationship separate. The cited passage supplies no judicial finding of responsibility, material support or operational direction. It also cannot establish whether other names or later Italian entries represent the same participants.

## US designation and alias scope: original documents (verified 20 November 2026)

[Federal Register notice 2025-20663](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-11-20/html/2025-20663.htm) designates **Informal Anarchist Federation/International Revolutionary Front** as a Foreign Terrorist Organization under INA section 219. The notice was dated **10 November 2025** and became effective upon publication **20 November 2025**. It explicitly lists **Brigata Augusto Masetti Federazione Anarchica Informale—Fronte Rivoluzionario Internazionale** and an English Augusto Masetti variant among the designated aliases. The separate [SDGT notice 2025-20666](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-11-20/html/2025-20666.htm) records the Executive Order 13224 designation.

**Interpretive boundary:** The US government's alias list provides an administrative naming link to the label used in Europol's 2022 Rome claim report. It does **not** authenticate that claim, establish a single stable command structure across listed cells, identify perpetrators or prove responsibility in the Rome case. US administrative designation is not an Italian criminal judgment.



## Italian judicial outcomes: convictions and acquittal (research update, 20 November 2026)

**Fossano 2006, final outcome in 2024.** Italian public broadcaster [Rai News, 24 April 2024](https://www.rainews.it/tgr/piemonte/articoli/2024/04/il-verdetto-della-cassazione-definitiva-la-condanna-a-23-anni-per-lanarchico-cospito--4e5b239b-520b-42ff-b3f4-f599c56349db.html) and [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/cronaca/2024/04/24/condanna-definitiva-per-cospito-a-23-anni-di-carcere_0ac8e4f8-21e5-47ce-9bc3-bf26877ddd9f.html) report that Italy's Court of Cassation rejected the appeals and finalised **23 years for Alfredo Cospito** and **17 years 9 months for Anna Beniamino** in the proceedings arising from the **2006** Fossano Carabinieri-school bombing. This is a **reported final judicial outcome**; the full Cassation judgment and precise operative findings have not yet been retrieved. Because the incident predates 2015, it must **not** be inserted as an additional 2015–2025 Europol attack.

**Adinolfi shooting 2012.** Rai reports a **2014 conviction** of Cospito to **10 years 8 months** for the 2012 shooting of Ansaldo Nucleare executive Roberto Adinolfi, which was claimed under the **Nucleo Olga FAI/FRI** label. Keep the conviction, responsibility claim and cell/command relationship analytically separate; the original 2014 judicial reasons are not yet reviewed. This incident also predates the attack-study window.

**Valentina Speziale: final acquittal.** [ANSA, 23 March 2026](https://www.ansa.it/amp/sito/notizie/cronaca/2026/03/23/anarchici-arrestata-ma-assolta-in-processo-fai-fri-niente-risarcimento_b09b485c-138f-4b7b-98b8-0b7d3fea6d77.html) reports that Speziale was **finally acquitted** of alleged participation in FAI/FRI. Her later claim for compensation for wrongful detention was rejected; the reporting describes the courts' distinction between circumstances that initially supported suspicion and the absence of sufficient proof of participation. **A denial of detention compensation does not reverse the acquittal.** The original acquittal and compensation judgments remain to be retrieved.

**Evidence discipline:** The above are reported outcomes of Italian courts, not original judgments inspected in this update. Neither conviction of two individuals nor an administrative US alias list establishes a single central FAI/FRI command over every 2016 or 2022 Italian entry.


## Italian prosecutor-general's original official report (20 November 2026)

The [Procura Generale di Torino, 2025 judicial-year report](https://pg-torino.giustizia.it/cmsresources/cms/documents/RELAZIONE%20PROCURATORE%20GENERALE.pdf), in its discussion of **case 18/22 R.G. Corte Assise di Appello**, supplies a primary prosecutorial account of the **Scripta Manent / Fossano 2006** proceedings. It states that the Court of Cassation's 6 July 2022 remittal led to a sentencing reconsideration, that the Constitutional Court's intervention permitted relevant mitigating factors, and that the Turin appeal court imposed **23 years on Cospito and 17 years 9 months on Beniamino**. The prosecutor-general confirms the Court of Cassation **rejected the prosecutor's appeal and declared the defence appeals inadmissible**, leaving the appeal sentences in place. The report also says the 2006 Fossano incident caused no deaths or injuries.

**Source distinction:** This is an **original report from a participating Italian prosecutorial authority**, stronger than secondary news coverage, but **not the full operative Cassation judgment or trial evidence**. The precise findings about cell membership, communications and operational command cannot be reconstructed from the sentencing summary alone. The 2006 act is historical context outside the 2015–2025 attack denominator.


## National source reconciliation for the 2016 parcels (audit correction, 20 November 2026)

The original [Italian intelligence report for 2016, printed pp. 69–70](https://www.senato.it/service/PDF/PDFServer/BGT/1021660.pdf) distinguishes the **7 June Parma** and **9 June Milan** parcel incidents from a direct perpetrator responsibility claim. It assesses a probable campaign relationship with the *Nucleo Danaus plexippus* communication. The event-level crosswalk documents the source locators and keeps both entries at **NO_VERIFIED_JOIN** for a merits judgment. The [Italian Ministry of Interior 2016 report, printed pp. 11–12](https://www.senato.it/service/PDF/PDFServer/DF/335766.pdf) separately records the **12 January Civitavecchia** device and the later **27 January FAI/FRI-labelled claim**. Neither claim nor intelligence attribution proves individual perpetrators or common operational command.
