# informal anarchist federation / international revolutionary front

Europol cites the Italian FAI/FRI as its example of anarchist terrorism in the 2023 report's category definition. That gives an ideological classification in an official report. The cited evidence does not independently date the federation's origin or establish a stable hierarchy, membership list or common direction across labels.

For 2022, Europol reports a Rome responsibility claim under the Augusto Masetti Brigade / FAI-FRI label. This is a **reported claim**. Part 1 records an adequate match for the foiled entry and keeps the claimant relationship separate. The cited passage supplies no judicial finding of responsibility, material support or operational direction. It also cannot establish whether other names or later Italian entries represent the same participants.
