# conspiracy of cells of fire

The US State Department's 2011 designation statement places the group's emergence in Greece in 2008 and describes a violent anarchist objective. It attributes attacks on commercial premises and targeting of civilians and officials to the group before the study period. That earlier agency account supplies historical context. It cannot establish a 2015–2025 trend. [US State Department designation statement](https://2009-2017.state.gov/r/pa/prs/ps/2011/10/175362.htm)

Europol names the Greek group as an example of anarchist terrorism in its 2023 category definition. This establishes Europol's classification of the named group. The definition alone establishes neither a specific 2015–2025 event match nor a link to Revolutionary Class Self-Defense. It also supplies no primary organisational declaration or judicial finding for this profile. Changes in structure and event-specific claims during the study period remain unresolved in these records.

Source [TE-SAT 2023, left-wing and anarchist terrorism definition](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1694f1b5-9a38-11ee-b164-01aa75ed71a1.0001.01/DOC_1)
