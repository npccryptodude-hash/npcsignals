# conspiracy of cells of fire

## Historical context and classification

The [US State Department's 2011 designation statement](https://2009-2017.state.gov/r/pa/prs/ps/2011/10/175362.htm) places the group's emergence in Greece in 2008 and describes a violent anarchist objective. It attributes attacks on commercial premises and targeting of civilians and officials to the group before the study period. This earlier agency account supplies historical context; it cannot establish a 2015–2025 trend.

Europol names the group as an example of anarchist terrorism in its [TE-SAT 2023 category definition](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1694f1b5-9a38-11ee-b164-01aa75ed71a1.0001.01/DOC_1). This is Europol's analytical classification, not proof of responsibility for every Greek left-wing/anarchist statistical entry.

## 2016 incident reported by Europol

[TE-SAT 2017, left-wing/anarchist chapter](https://www.europol.europa.eu/tesat/2017/4.LEFT-WING_AND_ANARCHIST_TERRORISM.html) reports that in October 2016 the group placed an explosive device outside the Athens residence of a prosecutor, causing substantial property damage. This is **Europol-reported attribution** of a specific event. The same report lists six Greek left-wing/anarchist terrorist attacks in 2016, but it does not attribute all six to this group.

Europol also reports that some Italian attacks in 2016 were assessed to have followed a call for action from imprisoned Greek anarchists. That is a cross-border **influence/solidarity assessment**, not evidence that this particular group commanded the Italian acts or that a coordinated cross-country campaign occurred. Europol expressly reports no coordinated campaign across Italy, Greece and Spain that year.

## Evidence limits

No primary organisational declaration or case-specific judicial finding has yet been verified for the **October 2016 Athens prosecutor-residence incident** in this profile. The later-reviewed Greek Supreme Court decisions concern other procedural histories and do not close this event-specific gap. The sources do not establish the individual participants, operational command, a 2015–2025 organisational chronology or any relationship to Revolutionary Class Self-Defense.

## Greek Supreme Court decisions: original national judgments (20 November 2026)

**Areios Pagos 1333/2018** ([official decision](https://www.areiospagos.gr/nomologia/apofaseis_DISPLAY.asp?apof=1333_2018&cd=ZSFD5EMZYDNB7AERPCS8ZTMB4EA8O2&info=%EF%BF%BD)) reproduces a judicial account describing the Conspiracy of Cells of Fire as an organised group with multiple participants, differentiated roles and alleged aims of carrying out terrorist acts. The passages must be read in their procedural context: a Supreme Court review reproducing findings and charges is not a finding that all persons ever named in investigations were members.

**Areios Pagos 442/2021** ([official decision](https://www.areiospagos.gr/nomologia/apofaseis_DISPLAY.asp?apof=442_2021&cd=CTRUXZM9XNV2644MP3Q7SKHVBMGMDY&info=%EF%BF%BD)) contains detailed national judicial material concerning alleged membership between January 2014 and a February 2015 arrest. Its case chronology and individual disposition must be mapped before counting a 2015 incident or claiming a particular defendant's final guilt.

**Areios Pagos 1223/2024** ([official decision](https://areiospagos.gr/nomologia/apofaseis_DISPLAY.asp?apof=1223_2024&cd=B08I1WMEC4SX8ILMY6IIMIQB1YM8RL&info=%EF%BF%BD)) concerns proceedings connected in the judgment's factual history to 2017 letter-bomb investigations. The Supreme Court on 28 June 2024 (published 10 October) **partially quashed** an appellate decision **only** as to acceptance of a post-offence good-behaviour mitigating circumstance and the consequent sentence, remitting that part for reconsideration. It rejected the prosecutor's cassation application in other respects. This **does not** mean the entire conviction was quashed; nor is the ultimate sentence after remittal established by this document. The court's recitation of 2017 events includes a reported claim of responsibility and police investigative connections, which are not automatically separate adjudications of organisational responsibility for each act.

**Attribution boundary:** These judicial materials strengthen the Greek legal-history section. They do **not** establish a direct judicial match for the **October 2016 prosecutor-residence incident** previously reported by Europol. That event's individual perpetrators and disposition remain unresolved.


## Case-level correction: Papademos 2017 (original text reviewed 20 November 2026)

[Areios Pagos 1223/2024](https://www.areiospagos.gr/nomologia/apofaseis_DISPLAY.asp?apof=1223_2024&cd=B08I1WMEC4SX8ILMY6IIMIQB1YM8RL&info=%EF%BF%BD) explicitly mentions the **25 May 2017** explosion in the vehicle carrying former Prime Minister Loukas Papademos. This supplies a precise **contextual event match** to register row **GR17-PAP**. The court's account identifies it as a separate investigation/trial, while the reviewed appeal case concerns weapons, explosives and other charges. The decision **does not adjudicate guilt for the Papademos attack**. The court also records an **irrevocable acquittal of a defendant on CCF-membership charges**; earlier police suspicion and prison visits must not be recast as membership. The attack-specific trial's ultimate result is still unverified. See [judgment–incident crosswalk](../JUDGMENT_INCIDENT_CROSSWALK.md).

## Papademos attack: later trial outcome, not organisational command (20 November 2026)

The earlier note describing the Papademos attack-specific trial as pending reflected the **earlier procedural time** recounted in Areios Pagos 1223/2024, a separate weapons/explosives case. [Kathimerini, 24 November 2023](https://www.kathimerini.gr/society/562746274/katadiki-gia-to-dema-vomva-ston-loyka-papadimo/) reports that **Konstantinos Giagtzoglou** was **convicted at first instance on 23 November 2023** for the **25 May 2017** letter-bomb attack, receiving an **11-year custodial sentence with suspended effect**. The court reportedly rejected attempted-murder classification in favour of an explosion endangering people. The original attack-trial judgment, appeal and finality are **not verified**. This is a **reported individual conviction**, not proof of CCF membership or direction; the separate Supreme Court ruling records an irrevocable acquittal on CCF-membership allegations in its own procedural scope. See [incident crosswalk](../JUDGMENT_INCIDENT_CROSSWALK.md).
