# nordic resistance movement

The 2021 Swedish case register preserves Europol-reported links to the Nordic Resistance Movement in a right-wing case. **Reported association** is the limit of that record. The link does not establish membership, assistance, direction or a finding about the organisation's objectives. The cited incident-linked record is insufficient to establish changes in structure or objectives across the period.
