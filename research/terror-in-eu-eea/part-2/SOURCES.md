# source register

## Category definitions

- [Europol, TE-SAT 2023, revised full edition](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1694f1b5-9a38-11ee-b164-01aa75ed71a1.0001.01/DOC_1), category definition boxes, annex pp. 79–80 and methodology pp. 88–90. Definitions describe analytical categories, and narrative examples need a specific join to national statistical returns.
- [Europol, TE-SAT 2026](https://www.europol.europa.eu/cms/sites/default/files/documents/EU-TE-SAT-2026.pdf), introduction p. 5, overview pp. 18–20 and category chapters. A source for the publisher's categories and published observations.
- [Directive (EU) 2017/541](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2017/541/oj/eng), art. 3. Legal elements and specified aims. The analysis preserves national classification and court outcomes as separate questions.
- [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Anarchism](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/anarchism/), §3.1, a scholarly overview of philosophical disagreement about violence and nonviolence.

## Left-wing and anarchist cases

- [Europol, TE-SAT 2023](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/1694f1b5-9a38-11ee-b164-01aa75ed71a1.0001.01/DOC_1), pp. 56–60, including p. 59 for the Rome claim. An official report can attest that a claim was reported. It does not verify who acted.
- [Europol, TE-SAT 2025](https://publications.europa.eu/resource/cellar/a8035d30-5241-11f0-a9d0-01aa75ed71a1.0001.01/DOC_1), pp. 42–43 and 62, and [TE-SAT 2026](https://www.europol.europa.eu/cms/sites/default/files/documents/EU-TE-SAT-2026.pdf), pp. 19–20, 38–39 and 47, for left-wing/anarchist reporting and aggregate outcomes.
- [Hellenic Police, 11 April 2025 announcement](https://www.astynomia.gr/2025/04/11/11-04-2025-anakoinosi-archigeiou-ellinikis-astynomias-schetika-me-ekrixi-pou-simeiothike-sti-leoforo-syngrou/), occurrence only. [Europol TE-SAT 2026](https://www.europol.europa.eu/cms/sites/default/files/documents/EU-TE-SAT-2026.pdf), p. 39, records the group claim.
- [US National Counterterrorism Center, Revolutionary Class Self-Defense, June 2026](https://www.odni.gov/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/CT-Guide-Revolutionary-Class-Self-Defense_508.pdf), pp. 1–2. Official assessment of aims, structure and two attributed cases within 2015–2025. The attribution's source is an intelligence agency.
