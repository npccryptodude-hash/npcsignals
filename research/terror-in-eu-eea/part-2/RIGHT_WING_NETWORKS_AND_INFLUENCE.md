# Right-wing terrorism: organisations, propaganda and individual cases

The right-wing statistical category is not an organisation. The cases below show why propaganda influence, a reported association, a judicially established act and operational direction must be treated as different propositions.

## Documented case anchors

| Case | Source-supported proposition | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| Sweden, 2021 | Europol reports links to Nordic Resistance Movement (NMR), SWE-R1 | Membership, assistance, direction and NMR decision-making |
| Slovakia, Bratislava, 2022 | Europol reports influence of Terrorgram publications, SK22-BRA-R1 | Any membership, direct communication, financing or command |
| Italy, Macerata, 2018 | Europol's 2018 cohort reports one completed right-wing terrorist attack; Part 1 identifies Macerata | A group-level attribution is not supplied by the category |
| France, Milice Rouge project, 2025 | French Senate recounts PNAT investigation of a disrupted project attributed to a group described as supremacist and neo-Nazi, p6-milice-rouge | Individual roles, adjudicated guilt and a final judgment |
| France, Saint-Étienne project, 2025 | Senate reports PNAT terrorism-association qualification, p6-saint-etienne-pnat | Final merits outcome and any wider organisation link |

## Organisations and influence are separate

NMR is a named organisation. Terrorgram in the cited Slovak record is a publication/influence context; the source does not establish that it functioned as a directing organisation in that attack. The French records concern proceedings and official descriptions, not convictions. These distinctions prevent the category from becoming a list of presumed memberships.

The reported case anchors do not permit a Europe-wide estimate of the proportion of right-wing attacks committed by organisations. Nor do they establish a single common objective across the full category. Racist, antisemitic, anti-government and other ideological narratives must be attributed to the specific actors and primary documents that express them.

## Research tasks before historical synthesis

1. Obtain original national judicial or prosecutorial documents for Macerata, Bratislava and the Swedish case.
2. Separate NMR's documented organisational programme from the alleged actions of any individual reportedly linked to it.
3. Establish whether Terrorgram is documented as an influence, communications network, assistance network or operational directing body in each case.
4. Preserve investigation versus indictment versus conviction and procedural finality in the French 2025 cases.
5. Compare target selection, casualties and case outcomes only where the denominators and attribution stages are compatible.
