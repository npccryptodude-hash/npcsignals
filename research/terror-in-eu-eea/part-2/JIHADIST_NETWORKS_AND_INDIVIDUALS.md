# Jihadist networks and individuals: evidence of organisational involvement

This chapter distinguishes an ideological category from Islamic State (IS) membership, reported contact, online inspiration, financial assistance and operational direction. It uses the established Part 1 case registers, not an independent census of jihadist organisations.

## Case comparisons

| Case | Verified or attributed relationship | Evidence status | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Germany, ICE attack, 2021 | Court found a radical jihadist motive and a solo decision | Judicial finding in 2021 register, ICE-R1 | No membership or direction established in reviewed judgment summary |
| Denmark, Holbæk plot, 2021 | PET describes attempted-terrorism and financing conviction, and a separate assistance finding involving an IS fighter | PET account of appeal judgment, HOL-J1/HOL-R1 | No verified IS command of the attack plot |
| Hungary, 2021 | Europol reports declared allegiance to IS and communication with IS-associated people | Official reporting, KEC-R1 | Allegiance is not verified membership or operational direction |
| France, Strasbourg, 2022 | Europol attributes contact with IS members to investigators | Official reporting, FR22-STR-R1 | Contact details and judicial finding not verified |
| Germany, Solingen, 2024 | German court findings include IS membership; Europol separately records an IS claim and messaging | BGH final disposition J-SOL; Europol attribution R-SOL | Financing and case-specific operational command not established |
| Austria, Vienna Westbahnhof, 2025 | DSN reports contact with a purported ISKP member | Security assessment, p5-west-contact | Counterparty identity, financing and central command remain uncertain |
| Poland, 2025 preparation case | ABW alleges intended affiliation with an IS-linked organisation | Agency allegation, p3-pl-prep | Neither membership nor assistance established in a conviction |

## What the cases permit

IS is a named jihadist terrorist organisation in Europol's classification. The cases illustrate several different possible relationships to the organisation and its affiliates, not a single command structure. Even a final judgment establishing membership does not necessarily decide whether central leadership ordered a particular attack.

The category counts in [the statistical overview](STATISTICS_AND_NORWAY.md) measure reported attacks, not group membership. The annual arrest series measures suspects rather than attack incidents or convictions. This chapter does not estimate the share of attacks directed by IS.

## Further documentary work

- Reopen original judicial reasons for Solingen, Holbæk and the 2021 German ICE case, distinguishing factual findings from prosecutorial assertions.
- Identify original sources for any claimed organisational objective, rather than inferring it from an attack target or a propaganda claim.
- Trace each reported contact's source and procedural status; avoid equating online exchange, declared allegiance and membership.
- Separate IS central organisation, affiliates, purported affiliates and unidentified supporters.
