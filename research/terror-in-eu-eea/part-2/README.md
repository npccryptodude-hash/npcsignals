# ideologies, organisations and objectives behind the numbers

The first part of this investigation checks terrorism statistics for 2015–2025. This part asks what sources can tell us about the people and groups behind particular entries, and what those sources say about their aims. The research area includes 31 countries. Europol's published reporting populations cover their respective EU scopes.

An official category tells you how an entry was reported. A court can establish facts about a specific case. A group statement can show what someone publicly claimed. Each answers a different question.
