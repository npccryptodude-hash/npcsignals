# Organisational claims, inspiration and court outcomes: 2022 case contrasts

This case comparison is drawn from Part 1's [2022 proceedings and relationship register](../2022/proceedings_relationships.csv). It does not assign new Europol statistical entries, revise national legal classifications or assert that the selected cases are representative.

## Four different evidence patterns

| Case | What the existing source record supports | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Italy, Rome (IT22-ROM) | Europol reports a responsibility claim attributed to the Augusto Masetti Brigade / FAI-FRI label (IT22-ROM-R1). | Identity of the actors, actual group membership, assistance or operational direction. |
| Greece, 2022 (GR22-MAR) | Europol reports a claim under the name Active in Solidarity Anarchists (GR22-MAR-R1). | Independent verification of the claimant, command structure or continuity with other named anarchist groups. |
| Slovakia, Bratislava (SK22-BRA) | Europol reports influence from Terrorgram publications (SK22-BRA-R1). | Membership in a named organisation, direct communication, assistance, financing or command. |
| France, Strasbourg (FR22-STR) | Europol attributes contact with IS members to investigators (FR22-STR-R1). | Details of the contact, a judicial finding of membership or operational direction. |

These four records illustrate why the following statements must remain distinct:

- **A responsibility claim** documents a public assertion, not necessarily the identity of the perpetrators.
- **Ideological influence** documents a reported connection to ideas or publications, not an organisational relationship.
- **Reported contact** documents an association at the source's stated evidentiary level, not an instruction to commit a specific act.
- **A judicial finding** is bounded by the court's charges, reasoning, procedural stage and finality; none of the four relationship rows independently supplies a final judicial finding of organisational command.

## An additional judicial boundary: Iceland

The same 2022 register records an Icelandic case (IS22-INV) in which the Supreme Court remitted the matter in March 2026. The June 2026 appellate judgment acquitted on attempted-terrorism and complicity counts while convicting on serious weapons offences; a July notice reports the prosecution did not seek further appeal permission. This illustrates that an investigation, charge or preliminary judgment cannot be treated as a final terrorism conviction. The Icelandic case lies outside the EU attack-statistics denominator and must not be added to Europol's 2022 attack count.

## Research consequences

1. For FAI/FRI and the Greek claim label, obtain the original statement where lawful and available, and test its provenance separately from the event and the police investigation.
2. For Terrorgram-related influence, distinguish propaganda consumption, network participation, communication and coordinated direction.
3. For alleged IS contacts, seek judicial or investigative documents defining what the contacts actually did.
4. Record changes in national judicial outcomes without overwriting the historical Europol reporting classification.
5. Keep the selected cases outside any calculation of organisation-directed attack rates; the dataset is not complete enough to supply a denominator.
