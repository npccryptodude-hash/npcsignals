## Evidence tiers

- **Primary court record:** establishes the precise court finding and procedural status, not facts outside the ruling.
- **Primary executive action:** establishes a designation or sanction and its legal scope, not a criminal conviction.
- **Official intelligence or police assessment:** establishes what the agency assesses or reports; contested underlying facts require independent support.
- **Official report of a claim:** establishes that a claim was reported, not necessarily who acted.
- **Unverified:** missing original source, unknown procedural finality, or unresolved linkage.
