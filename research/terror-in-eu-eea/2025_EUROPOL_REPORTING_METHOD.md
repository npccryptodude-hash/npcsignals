# National reporting and the Europol crosswalk — 18 November 2026

Europol's TE-SAT series page says the agency prepares the assessment from information provided and verified by Member States and partners, including qualitative and quantitative contributions from Eurojust and Member States (eu-series-method). The 2026 report's introduction explains the national legal basis and the distinct treatment of violent extremism as context. Its p. 19 narrative and pp. 20 and 47 tables establish the published country, category and outcome totals (eu-full). The narrative's examples provide some incident identities.

Article 7 of the Europol Regulation provides for a national unit and a liaison framework; it also permits direct contact by competent authorities under national conditions (eu-reg7). This does not establish which French or Belgian office filed a specific row, the transmission channel for the annual return, or a mandatory case-level schema. The Commission's answer E-002096/2023 explains that Europol produces the report on its own responsibility, the Commission participates in methodology consultation through the Advisory Board, and the Commission is not in a position to double-check the report's underlying facts and data (eu-commission-method). Directive 2017/541 sets minimum legal rules. The 2019 methodological annex records the historical Council mandate and distinguishes national terrorist reporting from extremist examples (eu-method2019). Use the 2019 annex as historical evidence, without assuming every procedural detail applies in 2026.

Public searches of the Europol series materials, European Parliament questions and Council-document references using TE-SAT, ENFOPOL 65 and 8196/2/06 located descriptions of the framework. No public 2025 event-level national return, coding template, review log, decision rule for near-boundary failed/foiled events, or exact submission freeze was located. Court data, intelligence statistics, successful interventions and statistical incident rows have different meanings.

An explicit dated 2026 report example with compatible category and outcome, or sufficiently distinctive independent national evidence and an unambiguous published EU cell, can support an analytical match. A national conviction by itself establishes a legal disposition. A matching total, apparent motive or compatible method by itself leaves the statistical match unresolved. A published national contribution, correction or official named reference giving country, code and outcome would resolve the weaker joins.
