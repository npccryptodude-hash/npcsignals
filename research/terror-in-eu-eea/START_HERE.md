# terror in eu/eea

status: foundation / research in progress  
created: 2026-11-18  
coverage: 27 eu member states plus norway, iceland and liechtenstein  
historical window: 2015–2025; 2026 developments tracked separately

## purpose

build a verifiable, source-linked research database describing terrorism across the eu/eea: incidents, victims, ideology classifications, actors, organisations, documented links, financing, institutional responses and uncertainties.

start with an all-category overview, then investigate jihadist terrorism in greater depth based on the documented record. no predetermined conclusion about any ideology, religion, population or institution.

## status and limitations

this is a schema and source foundation, not a completed dataset or publication-ready finding. eu-wide europol figures must not be labelled eu/eea totals. non-eu eea countries require independently checked records. historical membership changes, especially the uk's departure from the eu, must be annotated.

no medium publication is authorised by this research setup.
