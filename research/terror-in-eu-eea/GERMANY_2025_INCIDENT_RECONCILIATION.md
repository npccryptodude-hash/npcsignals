## update — 2026-11-18, superseding early berlin assessment

Three German 2025 candidate incidents now have separate records: Munich (13 February), Berlin Holocaust Memorial (21 February), and Bielefeld (18 May). One of the four completed German attacks in Europol's 2025 reporting remains unidentified; individual event-to-Europol matching still requires the original annex.

Berlin: the Kammergericht's 5 March 2026 judgment found prolonged online preparation by an IS member and imposed a 13-year sentence (not final at announcement); a separate alleged accomplice was indicted in August 2026, announced 9 September. Thus the early 2025 statement that no direct network connection was identified is outdated, not a reliable current conclusion. See incidents/DE_2025_02_21_BERLIN_HOLOCAUST_MEMORIAL.md.

Bielefeld: 18 May 2025, four life-threatening injuries; federal prosecutor alleged historic IS membership; WDR reports life imprisonment on 1 June 2026 and defence intention to appeal. See incidents/DE_2025_05_18_BIELEFELD.md.

For these cases, separate confirmed court findings, prosecution allegations and journalistic court reporting. Do not conflate former IS membership, online preparation by a member, and centrally directed operations.
