# Calendar-year 2015 baseline — 18 November 2026

Europol released **TE-SAT 2016** on 19 July 2016 for calendar year 2015. It was published for EU28 including the UK. The full publisher PDF was downloaded on 18 November 2026: 60 pages, 4,038,824 bytes, DOI **10.2813/525171**, SHA-256 `45ebdbaac89072e05c581d441210bf42fa36e470100a3799455b3cf428d40fa5`. The EU Publications Office's later repository release date is 26 April 2017. [Source register](sources.csv).

Annex 1 p. 44 gives UK 103, France 73, Spain 25, Greece four, Italy four and Denmark two. The overview on p. 10 gives France **72** and **50 separatist** events. The annex gives France **73**, including **47 separatist**. Annex country and category sums both reconcile to 211. The overview values survive in [statistics.csv](statistics.csv). The UK's undifferentiated row precludes a national zero claim for any ideology. No complete split of the 211 into completed, failed and foiled was located. Five cases in [attacks.csv](attacks.csv) have a supported completed allocation; the remaining 206 lack a keyed outcome in this reconstruction. That is an allocation limit, not an estimate of how many were actually completed.

Annex 2 p. 45 gives **1,077 arrests** in 19 countries: 687 jihadist, 67 left-wing, 11 right-wing, 168 separatist, zero single issue and 144 unspecified. The UK contributes 134 unspecified arrests. Methodology pp. 51–53 describes nationally confirmed returns, judicially warranted arrests and national interpretations under the EU framework decision. Eurojust proceedings, contextual cases and arrests are distinct from attack counts.

The p. 10 overview gives **151 victim deaths** (France 148, Denmark two, Greece one) and **more than 360 injuries**. The jihadist overview and chapter give **150 deaths**, including 148 in France and two in Denmark. Annex 1's narrative states **148** jihadist deaths at apparent aggregate level. Its injury lower bound is **more than 350**, compared with **more than 250** in the overview. These are differently phrased lower bounds, not exact contradictory point counts. The named November Paris series has **130 deaths and 368 injuries** in the original edition. The all-ideology injury lower bound is consistent with that case count and cannot be used as an exact number. All 151 headline deaths can be located in fixed EU27. A comparable fixed-EU27 aggregate injury count remains unknown.

The [coverage matrix](coverage.csv) has 31 countries. The [crosswalk](crosswalk.csv) has **nine adequately supported slots, three candidates and 199 unidentified**. One representative November slot is counted as a lower bound because the series is confirmed in the French 15, while a key mapping individual sites to every assigned slot remains unavailable. Verviers and the Berlin attack appear in the narrative despite absent attack rows for those countries. They are separately documented as context.
