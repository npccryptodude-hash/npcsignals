## key methodological result
There is a real national-vs-Europol classification discrepancy for France. Keep separate national event universe, Europol counted universe, and legal case universe; never force a one-to-one join without explicit official reconciliation. Dublin additionally demonstrates that multiple physical acts may be prosecuted in one case but have distinct incident statuses.
