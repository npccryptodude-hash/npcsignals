# Statistical reconciliation of the 2015–2025 research foundation — 18 November 2026

The [consolidated series](COMPARABILITY_2015_2025.md) spans all 11 reference years. Its [CSV](data/eu_statistical_reconciliation_2015_2025.csv) is an index to source-attributed aggregates and statistical slots, not a row-level count of physical attacks. The existing archived series and year files remain intact. The [entry page](START_HERE.md) lists the annual baselines. Country grids cover 31 jurisdictions; absent data remain unreported or unknown unless a scoped zero is verified.

## Reconciliation controls

- Original totals come from the year reports, including historical UK rows through 2019. The five UK attack subtractions are 103, 76, 107, 60 and 64. The corresponding arrest subtractions are 134, 149, 412, 273 and 281. Where documented, other Northern Ireland observations are subsets, external measures or of unresolved overlap. None are subtracted again.
- The annual source-total fields equal the sum of adequate, candidate and unidentified slots. A candidate slot can have more than one possible identity. An adequate slot does not establish criminal guilt, operational direction or a single physical act. A multi-site case can have a known minimum allocation and unverified detailed grouping.
- The later-official and occurrence-year series are independent of the original source-total field. The numerical adjustments do not overwrite annual attack registers or casualty series.
- For 2015, actual bytes of the full publisher PDF were obtained and the reported locators inspected. The hash identifies those bytes and is not evidence that unrelated claims in the PDF were checked. For 2025 the Phase 7 audit records the retrieval limits at that stage. Phase 18 subsequently retrieved the complete publisher PDF and verified the statistical tables. See [source integrity](audit/EVIDENCE.md) and [Phase 19 remediation](audit/PHASE_19_REMEDIATION.md).

## Outstanding uncertainty

2015's unexplained 193 chart value, 2016's incompatible injury subtotals, the identified candidate for the 2020 deletion, the unkeyed French retrospective additions and the incomplete 2025 reconstruction remain open. The statistical foundation supports qualified reporting of published and derived EU figures. Coverage and slot evidence do not support a definitive 31-country census. A source's original ideology or outcome code remains a statistical classification even where national charging or legal findings use different definitions.
