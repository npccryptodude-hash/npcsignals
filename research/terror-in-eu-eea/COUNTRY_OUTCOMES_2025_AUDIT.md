## ideological attribution is not organisational direction

Europol's 2024 report explicitly states that all six completed jihadist attacks that year were perpetrated by lone attackers not directed or instructed by a specific terrorist group; one attack was claimed by IS. A claim of responsibility is not itself proof of command-and-control.

2024 original Europol TE-SAT p. 23: https://tpcoe.gov.pl/download/11/708/EUTE-SAT2025.pdf

For 2025 the reviewed digest says nine jihadist attacks were completed: eight stabbings and one vehicle ramming. It also reports several IS allegiance pledges, with little evidence of direct operational orders in most cases. This is not an incident-level verified statement for every attack and must not be automatically attached to individuals.
