# uk measurement register — 2026-11-18

status: source-linked extraction, not harmonised terrorism incident counts

## northern ireland

police service of northern ireland / nisra publishes security situation statistics, including shootings, bombings, security-related deaths, paramilitary assaults and section 41 arrests. the periods are generally financial years (april to march). these indicators are **not equivalent** to europol completed/failed/foiled terrorist attacks.

primary sources:
- https://www.psni.police.uk/official-statistics/security-situation-statistics
- https://www.psni.police.uk/sites/default/files/2025-05/Security%20Situation%20Statistics%20to%20March%202025.pdf
- https://www.nisra.gov.uk/news/paramilitary-assaults-fall-lowest-level-1983

the 2025/26 nisra release reports 8 shooting incidents and 19 bombing incidents; 2024/25 reported 18 and 5 respectively. no direct ideological classification should be assigned without incident-specific evidence.

## great britain

home office terrorism act statistics cover arrests and other policing measures, not a census of terrorist attacks. their published great britain scope excludes northern ireland. the release to june 2026 reports 3,867 terrorism-related arrests in the year ending june 2026, compared with 234 in the prior year. the home office attributes the dramatic increase to arrests linked to supporting palestine action after proscription on 2025-07-05. of 3,867 arrests, only 34 were under section 41 of the terrorism act; most were under other legislation. do not interpret this spike as evidence of a corresponding increase in completed terrorist attacks or jihadist activity. record legal and policy context and potential subsequent revisions.

primary source:
- https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/operation-of-police-powers-under-tact-2000-to-june-2026/operation-of-police-powers-under-the-terrorism-act-2000-and-subsequent-legislation-arrests-outcomes-and-stop-and-search-great-britain-to-june-202

## calendar-year national-security series

a 2026-04-14 written parliamentary statement gives northern ireland national security attacks as: 2017=5, 2018=1, 2019=5, 2020=0, 2021=0, 2022=1, 2023=1, 2024=0, 2025=0. this is a separate measure from psni security situation shootings/bombings and from europol attack totals. the statement separately reports 12 bombing incidents and 9 shooting incidents in calendar year 2025, illustrating why calendar-year and financial-year series must not be mixed.

primary source:
- https://questions-statements.parliament.uk/written-statements/detail/2026-04-14/hlws1514

## harmonisation rule

each numeric row must specify territory, period start/end, period type, metric, definition and source. retain distinct series unless equivalence has been demonstrated. national security attacks, shootings, bombings, terrorism-related arrests, prosecutions and europol terrorist attacks must not be summed as one measure.
