The [2016 geography audit](GEOGRAPHY_AUDIT.md) and [2017–2025 comparison](../2017/COMPARABILITY_2017_2025.md) trace definitions and historical adjustments. The 2016–2019 editions include the UK. Fixed EU27 holds geography constant, but not coding, reporting completeness or national definitions. The sensitivity calculation changes only the preceding documented 2020–2023 observations, assuming distinct late French entries. Neither the later Berlin and Nice casualty counts nor national court classifications are numeric revisions to 2016 attack statistics. No new official 2016 numeric revision was verified. The existing 2020 Berlin A100 candidate is separate from the 2016 Breitscheidplatz case. French retrospective cohorts retain their original provenance.

The 2016 victim and attack scopes differ in the UK, and case and annual injury figures are internally incompatible. Outcome proportions remain incomplete. An arrest count concerns suspected offences and cannot be read as a count of attackers. A geography difference is not evidence of an underlying trend.
