## Victim counts and internal incompatibility

The report gives **142 deaths and 379 injuries** across EU28 and **135 deaths and 374 injuries** from jihadist events. All 13 jihadist events are in fixed EU27. Footnote 3 on p. 10 identifies the seven other deaths as six linked to paramilitary violence in Northern Ireland and the murder of a UK MP. Thus the edition's headline death count outside the UK is 135. The murder of Jo Cox appears in the victim context, while the UK attack annex comprises only Northern Ireland-related entries. The measures do not share identical coverage.

Specific injury counts on pp. 23–24 are **340 for Brussels, 201 for Nice and 56 for Berlin**. Their sum alone is 597 and exceeds both published injury aggregates. No verified common case-level injury definition was found. Preserve these observations, the official headline figures and later national counts separately. The [statistics register](statistics.csv) carries those measures, including later Nice figures of 86 deaths and 458 injuries in a Justice Ministry-hosted research report and the 13th Berlin death recognised by the city in 2021. Keep the contemporary Nice 85 and Berlin 12 unrevised. The Brussels sentencing coverage records 35 murder victims within the judicial scope; the contemporary Europol combined case count of 32 is retained.
