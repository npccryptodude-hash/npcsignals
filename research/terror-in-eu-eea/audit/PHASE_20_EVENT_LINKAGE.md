## German 2020 reclassification

TE-SAT 2021 p. 44 n. 52 ties the 18 August A100 event to a dated joint police and prosecution notice, supporting the original completed jihadist allocation. The Landgericht docket `521 Ks 1/21` and BGH `4 StR 329/22` identify the same national proceeding and final disposition. TE-SAT 2023 p. 9 n. 4 records withdrawal following a German court ruling. It supplies no incident date, venue, court, docket or amendment key.

The Berlin Senate's response 19/21271 explains that its KPMD-PMK figures count cases when known and associate them with the offence date. Its grouping rule can count multiple acts or investigations as one case. The historical tables are aggregates without event IDs. Bundestag 20/3946 mentions A100 in an opposition motion and supplies neither executive reporting nor a statistical correction. Searches in Europol, Bundestag, Berlin parliament, BKA and BGH discovery surfaces by event date, court docket and revision/reregistration language yielded no public revision key. The independently documented national court case increases specificity on the national side. Removing the A100 entry itself remains an inference. ## France and Germany 2025

**France.** PNAT full-year activity booklet (bundle PDF p. 27, printed PNAT p. 1) explicitly includes Apt, Mulhouse and Lyon and reports seven foiled on that segment. The gendarmerie research note 125 pp. 1–2 dates all three, draws on public reporting and has an imprecise regional label for Mulhouse. The DNRT transcript of 16 October gives two completed jihadist events in its account, identifies Mulhouse and describes an earlier southern incident consistent with Apt. It predates year-end and Lyon's reported formal investigation of the transferred suspect on 28 October. The reported extension of Lyon's judicial investigation to terrorist offences took effect on 9 October, before the hearing. Thus the hearing's date alone cannot explain the omission. Its six preventive arrests use a different unit from PNAT's seven foiled and Europol's eight foiled. TE-SAT 2026 pp. 19, 47 report two completed and eight foiled and provide no candidate-specific link for the French completed cell. No public French docket for the three cases or submission key was confirmed from the prosecutor, parliamentary or published court records inspected. Case-level procedural descriptions retain their source class and date. No alternative pair is eliminated. [French reconciliation](../FRANCE_2025_PNAT_VS_EUROPOL_DISCREPANCY.md) · [Phase 20 linkage register](phase20_event_linkage.csv).

**Essen.** NRW's 2025 security report pp. 28 and 216 expressly places Essen and Bielefeld among five jihadist-motivated attacks in Germany. It describes Essen within NRW's religious-ideology PMK context on p. 56. The federal government in Bundestag 21/4296 p. 18, answer 29, lists Munich, Berlin, Bielefeld and Essen in reply to a question about 2025 incidents. Answer 28 describes evolving classifications. The 6 May 2026 answer 28, document 21/5846 p. 16, concerns prevented events and does not update the completed roster. NRW supplies no list of the other three or explanation for the numeric difference with the federal answer and EU annex. Scope, submission cutoff and coding are live alternatives. No fifth incident is identified from this comparison. The visible roster is therefore insufficient to infer Essen's membership from a four-to-four match. The NRW response 18/16237 gives age 17 in October 2025. The reproduced OLG Düsseldorf announcement for `III-5 St 2/26` puts age at 17 years and eight months on the offence date and reports a first-instance juvenile sentence of nine years on 22 September 2026. Public OLG/NRWE and BGH docket checks located no original full reasons or public revision disposition. The new state classification strengthens independent national corroboration. Europol's age observation on p. 27 uses qualified wording and supplies no case-level age reference. Essen remains a candidate. [NRW annual report, pp. 28, 56, 216](https://www.im.nrw/system/files/media/document/file/vsb2025_online_0.pdf) · [Federal answer 21/4296 p. 18](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/042/2104296.pdf#page=18) · [Federal answer 21/5846 p. 16](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/058/2105846.pdf#page=16) · [Essen judicial chronology](../incidents/DE_2025_09_05_ESSEN.md).

## French retrospective cohorts

- **Right-wing, 2021.** The gendarmerie research centre note 66 pp. 3–4 gives dated disruption or investigative milestones and identifies alleged plot or group labels. Those milestones can differ from attack-reference dates. Thionville/Honneur et Nation was described as a suspected plot after early-May arrests. The 2025 court coverage identifies a related prosecuted plot and reports a different target locality in the case narrative, underlining that an investigation label can cover evolving allegations. Projet Azur's late-October formal investigation has a national case label. Court reporting dates a first-instance conviction for terrorist criminal association and another offence on 15 October 2026. Full reasons and appeal status were not verified. Recolonisation France appears as a November arrest series without a distinct validated foiled-event date or public docket. Treat it as a search lead with no proposed slot claim. The published French parliamentary answer 34314 is from March 2021 and its numbers span prior years. It cannot identify the retrospective 2021 cohort. None of the three has a public submission key. The alleged cases are not three proven distinct statistics.
