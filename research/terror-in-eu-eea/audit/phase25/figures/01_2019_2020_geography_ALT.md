# phase 25 — figure 01 accessible alternative

**title:** 2019–2020: geography changes the comparison.

**unit:** reported terrorist attack entries in europol's annual reporting cohorts. this is not a count of all physical incidents.

| observation | reported entries | scope and status |
|---|---:|---|
| 2019 | 119 | original EU28 cohort, including the UK |
| 2019 | 55 | fixed EU27 comparison, UK row excluded |
| 2020 | 57 | original EU27 cohort |
| 2020 | 56 | later official revised observation, not the original 2020 cohort |

**calculation:** the 2019 UK row accounts for 64 entries. 119 − 64 = 55. the fixed-geography original-cohort comparison is therefore 55 in 2019 versus 57 in 2020. the later official revision for 2020 is 56.

**source locators:** TE-SAT 2020 pp. 84–85; TE-SAT 2021 pp. 12, 99–100; TE-SAT 2023 p. 9 n. 4. see [the annual edition source guide](../SOURCE_GUIDE.md).

**limitations:** the adjustment aligns geographic scope, not national coding practices. it cannot establish a change in the actual terrorism threat. the later revision must remain visually and textually distinct. the original 2015 chart discrepancy (193 versus 211) is outside the plotted period.

**accessibility:** all values and caveats are presented as text above. the SVG has a title and descriptive alternative. the dedicated [360 px version](mobile/01_2019_2020_geography.svg) was rendered and checked for this review. any final dissemination export remains subject to human review.
