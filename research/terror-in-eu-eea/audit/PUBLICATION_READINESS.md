# Publication-readiness decisions

Internal assessment. The reports may describe the official returns, their geographic adjustments and limitations without estimating complete European incidence.

| Output | Decision | Permitted statement or remaining requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Annual statistical tables | Ready for qualified publication | Name the edition, geography and reported measure. Attach the discrepancy register and retain raw anomalies and working transcriptions. |
| Fixed EU27 comparison | Ready for qualified publication | Report country-row sums and explain the excluded UK components. Compare reporting cohorts under a fixed geographic footprint, with annual differences in inclusion and national classification disclosed. |
| Historical trend visualisation | Requires specific corrections | Use titled reporting-cohort values and distinguish official revisions, alternative chart readings and analytical adjustments. State the effect of UK removal and mark retrospective returns. A causal, physical-incidence or organisational trend is unsupported. |
| Incident-level database | Requires further primary evidence | Publish narrowly verified extracts with confidence flags if authorised. A census or inference treating unidentified slots as no events is unsupported. |
| Judicial findings | Ready for qualified publication at case level | Limit claims to the decision scope and verified stage. Further primary evidence is needed for source-limited, pending or uncertain finality cases. |
| Organisational relationships | Requires further primary evidence | Case-specific claims, allegiance, membership, communication and assistance can be reported at their proven status. Cross-year trends in command or membership lack a consistent denominator. |
| Financial-support findings | Not supportable from available sources as a general series | Case-specific court or official dispositions, such as the Danish agency's report of an appeal finding, have their own evidence status. Missing findings do not demonstrate no financial support. |
| Thirty-one-country coverage claim | Not supportable from available sources as a census | The research grid covers 31 countries. National event coverage is partial, and the UK and EFTA data cannot be pooled into an equivalent EU series. |

## Limitations and priorities

1. Retain reproducible report references for all graphics. Recover publisher copies of the three mirror-based editions if possible and record any meaningful text or layout changes. Request no external contact under the current scope. Verify 2025's arrest-age denominator only against independent public documentation.
2. Prioritise keyed records for the German 2020 deletion, the French cohorts and the separate October 2023 32/34 conflict. Date-assigned estimates are conditional on cohort membership and unresolved overlap. The original 2019–2020 comparison moves from 119 to 57; geography-normalised it moves from 55 to 57. Neither comparison is a causal estimate.
3. Keep all death and injury observations at their published scope, with definition and date. Unknown inclusion thresholds, different victim definitions, and the 2016 contradiction preclude a pooled casualty series.
4. Preserve the distinction between outcome allocations and outcome margins, and statistical slots and physical acts. Seek national submissions and appeal decisions for the 2025 unresolved mappings. Maintain an observable difference between exact ID uniqueness and entity resolution across registers.
5. Obtain the missing legal reasons and case-level finality documents before releasing stronger claims about organisation, responsibility or financing. Keep arrest counts distinct from attackers, convictions and unique people across years. Keep Northern Ireland measures, national legal classifications, absent rows and verified zeros in their own reporting scopes.
