# Source integrity and judicial evidence

## Statistical documents

[Source integrity ledger](source_integrity.csv) identifies each exact full report opened in this audit, the supplying host, printed statistical locators, byte size and SHA-256. The publisher PDFs for reference years 2015–2021 matched the hashes in the annual source registers. The revised 2022 copy from Polish Police matched the recorded hash, and the 2024 copy from that institution and 2023 third-party reproduction were reopened. These mirrors are full report copies whose titles, editions and statistical pages were checked. No byte-equivalence to a fresh Europol download is asserted for the mirrors. The 2022 baseline further documents the superseded June edition and its unchanged annex values. For each PDF the digest identifies bytes reviewed in this audit. It does not date the original publication or independently authenticate an edition.

The recovered publisher TE-SAT 2026 is 58 PDF pages, DOI 10.2813/5264545. The executive summary listing is a separate publication object and is inadequate as the locator for annex p. 47. Fresh verification covered the attack tables (pp. 19–20, 47–49), arrest table (p. 22), retrospective note and selected case claims. Phase 7's 109 returned responses out of 134 URLs continue to measure technical access in that pass. No general claim is made that all retrieved linked legal documents or every annual-source URL were reverified. An HTTP success can return a landing page or a challenge, as with the Nice cassation URL in this audit.

## Case sample

| Case | Claim supported and evidence class | Limit for release |
|---|---|---|
| Paris, November 2015 | Paris appellate court's civil release identifies the separate criminal judgment and scope of civil liability. Criminal sentencing detail is supported separately by trial reporting. [2015 sources](../2015/sources.csv), FR-PAR-CIV and FR-PAR-VER. | Civil liability cannot establish each criminal count, finality for every defendant, or the extent of organisational command. Europol's organisational attribution and IS's claim require separate labels. |
| Brussels 2016 | Reported assize verdict distinguishes terrorist murders and terrorist group participation. Belga attributes the expiry of the cassation deadline without a filing to the court spokesperson. [2016 sources](../2016/sources.csv) and [Belga, 2 October 2023](https://www.belganewsagency.eu/no-appeals-filed-in-brussels-terrorism-trial-making-all-rulings-final). | Finality is reported via the spokesperson; full criminal reasons not freshly inspected. The related airport and metro records share one trial and must not duplicate legal counts. |
| Nice 2016 | Annual register distinguishes the appeal judgments for terrorist criminal association and the recorded cassation disposition, docket 24-84.288. [2016 sources](../2016/sources.csv), FR-NIC-CAS. | Retrieval in Phase 18 returned a challenge page; no fresh verification of the decision text for this case. IS's claim is not evidence of direction or financing. Offences of association cannot be described as convictions for perpetrating the killings. |
| Berlin 2016 | TE-SAT 2017 pp. 23–24 reports the allegiance and claim and uses its contemporary casualty count. [2016 judicial register](../2016/proceedings_relationships.csv). | Keep the parliamentary reconstruction and later victim recognition separate from Europol. Public allegiance and attribution do not establish a chain of command. |
| Manchester 2017 | Central Criminal Court sentencing remarks verify Hashem Abedi's conviction and deliberate participation. Inquiry vol. 3 § 22.55 separately assesses probable assistance/training. § 22.56 limits the evidence about other family members' knowledge. [2017 sources](../2017/sources.csv), GB-MAN and GB-INQ. | The inquiry assesses probabilities and the conviction establishes individual liability. Neither establishes that a named organisation commanded the attack. |
| Barcelona/Cambrils 2017 | Europa Press reported the cassation disposition on 27 November 2023, giving 43 and 36 years for organisation and explosives-related offences. [2017 sources](../2017/sources.csv), ES-TS. | Use reported status. Directly inspect the full judgment for defendant-level publication. Those offences do not amount to convictions for perpetrating the killings. |
| Strasbourg 2018 | TF1 reports a 30-year appeal sentence and individual procurement assistance. The register records cassation filed with disposition unverified. [2018 sources](../2018/sources.csv), FR-STR-AP. | Preserve reported appellate status. Do not attribute individual assistance to IS on the basis of its responsibility claim. |
| The Hague 2018 | First-instance court release rejects terrorist intent and records compulsory treatment. NOS reports similar reasoning on appeal. [2018 sources](../2018/sources.csv), NL-HAG and NL-HAG-AP. | Appeal reporting is secondary and cassation status remains unverified. The original Europol statistics preserve their original classification. |
| Utrecht 2019 | National judgment ECLI:NL:RBMNE:2020:1046 is the primary first-instance source for terrorist intent in the annual register. [2019 sources](../2019/sources.csv), NL-UTR. | Current finality not established in this sample. Propaganda mentioning an attack cannot establish association or command. |
| Berlin A100 2020 | Berlin court's release for 521 Ks 1/21 confirms the express rejection of the Islamist-attack characterisation, lack of criminal responsibility and psychiatric placement. [2020 sources](../2020/sources.csv), DE-BER. | Release states the ruling lacked finality at announcement. A separate Europol identifier is needed to confirm the removal match. |
| ICE and Holbæk 2021 | German court-announcement lineage supports the ICE trial result, and subsequent finality is attributed to the reproduced BGH release 3 StR 244/23. Danish PET reports an appeal finding including financing in the Holbæk case. [2021 register](../2021/proceedings_relationships.csv). | Financing is a case-specific finding with an official agency as intermediary. Keep sender/recipient, assistance and command outside the claim where they lack their own evidence. |
| Essen 2022 | The BGH detention decision concerns the warrant and preparation allegation. The later juvenile judgment and finality in the annual register are reported. [2022 register](../2022/proceedings_relationships.csv). | A warrant finding describes the evidence at that stage. Do not turn it into a merits decision or conflate the case with Essen 2025. |
| Duisburg 2023 | Düsseldorf OLG judgment 6 StS 2/23 covers two acts. The primary database records finality. Europol's self-radicalisation account is an official attribution with a different purpose. [2023 sources](../2023/sources.csv), DE-DUI. | A shared judgment does not establish a one-physical-event statistical rule. Ideological evidence does not establish tasking or funding. |
| Solingen 2024 | BGH finality (3 StR 55/26) comes via a reproduction of the court announcement. Europol separately reports communications and encouragement. [2024 sources](../2024/sources.csv), DE-SOL and EU24. | Membership and direct communication are separate from command and financing. Cite the reproduction as such until the publisher original is obtained. |
| Munich, Bielefeld and others 2025 | Reopened TE-SAT 2026 p. 27 independently confirms what Europol reports: 57 injured in Munich and four critically injured in Bielefeld, with past and subsequent IS links described for the latter. [Judicial register](../data/jihadist_2025_judicial.csv) tracks court stages separately. | The Bielefeld injury figure is a severity subset, and the links describe Europol's assessment. Neither establishes command or financing. Existing legal-source finality flags remain case-specific. |

The source sample uses newly read passages and existing annual references where document access or scope limits the audit. A primary press statement proves what its issuer announced; a court's sentence or a finality notice supports a different proposition. Public claims and press reporting retain their source classes.
