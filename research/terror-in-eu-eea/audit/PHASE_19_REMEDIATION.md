## Publication assessment and phase 20

Annual tables and the fixed EU27 comparison remain suitable for qualified publication with edition, geography and reporting caveats. The BGH record improves the release status of A100's case-specific finality. A historical graphic should label the baseline, official later corrections and any analytical occurrence-year scenario with their respective provenance, and annotate the repeated 193 chart value. Injury counts, comprehensive incident reconstruction and organisation/command series retain the existing restrictions. Neither these statistics nor judicial finality alone establish actual attack rates, operational command or financing.

Prioritised public-evidence targets, with no outreach in this pass:

1. German correction record with a 2020 case identifier, and French and German 2025 incident submissions with dated event IDs, coding and age reference dates.
2. French national-to-Europol identifiers for the three right-wing backfills, the 26 separatist backfills, and the separately scoped October 2023 cases. Seek return dates and explicit counting rules.
3. Europol source data for the 2015 chart, the 2016 injury returns and the 2025 demographic return or erratum. A case-level definition and cutoff would allow a test of 2016 aggregation.
4. Primary appeal and finality documentation for Essen and The Hague, and other case-specific gaps from the Phase 18 register.

Next phase should target public event identifiers, versioned statistical returns and judicial dispositions. Aggregate similarities alone should have no effect on frozen confidence classes.
