# Phase 22 — Reproducible visualisation specification

Internal specification. Inputs are versioned in this research tree. Use reference years as integers, source-provenance in captions or hover detail, accessible labels alongside colour and explicit missing-value handling. Do not connect across missing official revisions. For an export, record the Git commit and output of both CSV comparisons in the [historical analysis](PHASE_22_HISTORICAL_ANALYSIS.md#readiness-and-validation).

| Visual | Source, population and unit | Reproducible construction | Caption and inference boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EU28 versus fixed EU27** | [Annual CSV](../data/phase22_annual_analysis.csv), 2015–2019 original and fixed geography, reported attack entries | Group two zero-based bars per year using original_attacks and fixed_eu27_attacks; label included_uk_attacks as the difference. | Geography subtraction leaves reporting practices intact. Bars measure edition entries; no harmonised frequency or EU27 share of underlying physical activity. |
| **Eleven-year EU27 reporting series** | Annual CSV, 2015–2025, reported entries and reported arrests on fixed geography | Use separate aligned panels for fixed_eu27_attacks and fixed_eu27_arrests. Label gaps in coverage and flag 2020 scope transition in the original series plus the 2023 cohort. Arrest panel flags original 426 versus annex sum 431. | Cohort observations and different units. No dual axis, arrest-to-attack ratio or inference of actual incident frequency. |
| **Official revisions and conditional sensitivity** | Annual CSV and [adjustment ledger](../2021/provenance.csv), EU27 statistical entries, focus 2020–2023 | Plot fixed baseline points and a distinct sensitivity series; display later official values as markers only where the revision field is populated. Show 193 as an annotation on a separate 2015 provenance note. | 56 and 18 are later official observations. Moves of 4 and 22 into earlier years and removal of 26 from 2023 are conditional. A missing revision marker has no zero value. |
| **Identification coverage** | Annual CSV, original geographic scope, three confidence counts per year | Draw 100% stacks by computing each count divided by original_attacks, with exact numerator and denominator in labels or accessible table. Display original cohort size and UK inclusion. | Coverage tracks slot identification, including candidates with alternatives. Missing identities imply neither missing events nor absence of events. |
| **Institutional reporting differences** | [Reporting chain](phase21_reporting_chain.csv), [denominators](phase21_denominators.csv) and Phase 21 case studies | Use a grouped institutional table or two compact diagrams. Only draw documented transitions as solid edges; render unknown national-to-EU joins explicitly. Show DE 5/4/4 and FR 3/2 with agency, period, and scope adjacent to values. | Distinct administrative, intelligence, prosecutorial and statistical units. Count differences do not identify omitted events or establish a direct data flow. |
| **Judicial and organisational evidence chains** | [Case sample](PHASE_22_ARREST_AND_JUDICIAL_MEASURES.md#case-sample-and-evidence-level) and its source IDs, selected cases | Use a case-level timeline with source type, procedural stage and dates. Show only documented relationship claims with predicate and evidence status. Keep allegations, official attribution, judgment and finality in separate labels. | An appeal announcement, inquiry assessment or responsibility claim establishes only its stated scope. Edges do not establish shared command, financing or a comparative organisational rate. |

No visual has been deployed or released. Visual assembly should use the exact counts in the CSV rather than rounded display percentages.
