Reassignment adds 22 completed events to 2022 (38 completed) and removes 26 completed events from 2023 (72 completed). Four belong to 2021, outside this comparison window. This scenario assumes each cohort entry is a distinct event with no other statistical allocation and makes no adjustment for undiscovered revisions. There is no verified event-level deduplication. These values are neither revised Europol figures nor a complete occurrence-date census. The published totals and crosswalks remain intact.
