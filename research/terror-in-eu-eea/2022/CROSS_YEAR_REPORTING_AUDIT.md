# French retrospective reporting audit — 18 November 2026

## What the editions establish

| Reference year | Edition | Evidence about French separatist records |
|---|---|---|
| 2021 | TE-SAT 2022 | Annex p. 82 reports 15 EU attacks. All five French attacks are jihadist. There is no French separatist allocation. |
| 2022 | TE-SAT 2023 | Annex p. 79 reports 28 EU attacks. France reports four jihadist and two right-wing attacks. There is no French separatist allocation in either inspected full version. |
| 2023 | TE-SAT 2024 | Annex p. 61 counts 70 completed French separatist events. Printed pp. 10–11 and 50 specify in footnotes that France reported four 2021 and 22 2022 attacks in 2024 and explicitly state that those data were absent from TE-SAT 2022 and 2023. |
| 2025 | TE-SAT 2026 | The previously verified p. 47 n. 44 gives the split of the 70 as four in 2021, 22 in 2022 and 44 in 2023. |

Sources EU21, EU22, EU22-JUN, EU23 and EU25 in [sources.csv](sources.csv). The 2024 edition itself establishes late submission and earlier absence for these data; the later footnote makes the occurrence-year split explicit. This is positive evidence of retrospective additions at the published cohort level. **No actual duplicate was verified.** The public tables cannot test whether any physical event appeared under an alternative category or administrative identity, or whether there are duplicates within the retrospectively submitted list. The national submission and row-level identifiers remain necessary for those checks.

There is a separate retrospective adjustment in TE-SAT 2023 p. 9 n. 4: France subsequently reported three foiled right-wing attacks for 2021 (15 originally, 18 in the later overview). The same footnote explains a 2020 change from 57 to 56 after a German court found a previously reported incident did not qualify. Neither adjustment refers to the Corsican cohort.

## National corroboration and limits

- The PNAT's 2025 activity booklet returns four Corsican terrorism referrals in 2021 and 22 in 2022, numerically consistent with the retrospective cohorts. It counts referrals and provides no row-level concordance with attack occurrences. For 2023 the booklet reports 74 referrals, whereas Europol gives 44 events occurring in 2023 in the 70-record cohort.
- National Assembly report 1466 note 41 records 64 attacks and fires between March 2022 and April 2023. The scope and period differ and provide no mapping.
- The Interior Ministry's answer to Senate question 03246 gives 32 facts for the 8–9 October 2023 series (15 + 16 in gendarmerie areas and one in the national police area), against 34 in TE-SAT 2024 p. 50. Destructions and attempted destructions are both in the French wording. The reply documents investigation and a public claim of responsibility; any individual criminal attribution requires its own evidence. The difference supplies no evidence of duplication and is outside the 26 older incidents.

The [cohort register](cross_year_events.csv) records year, edition, outcome, country, evidence and duplicate-review status. None of the 26 physical identities or exact dates could be verified against the submission. French parliamentary and prosecution records reviewed did not disclose a usable dated submission list. No speculative rows were created.
