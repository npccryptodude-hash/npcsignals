# thailand legal-case tracker

version: **0.5**

scope: long-tail legal consequences connected to the 2020 democracy-protest cycle.

## aggregate indicators

**CONFIRMED / QUALIFIED**

- in its **8 april 2026 UPR submission**, human rights watch stated that at least **1,469 people** believed to have been involved in 2020 democracy rallies were still being prosecuted for emergency-decree / covid-related offenses.
- HRW had reported the same 1,469 figure in earlier material. the archive therefore treats it as a **reported figure still cited in 2026**, not proof that a fresh 2026 recount produced the same total.
- other aggregate prosecution counts use different inclusion rules and can overlap with this group. they must not be added together mechanically.

## arnon nampa

role: prominent human-rights lawyer and democracy activist.

**CONFIRMED**

- HRW World Report 2026 says he faces **14 royal-defamation cases** tied to speeches and online commentary calling for monarchy reform.
- HRW reports repeated denial of bail.
- the UN working group on arbitrary detention called for his immediate release in august 2024.
- on 24 february 2026, he and three other democracy activists were convicted in another section 112 case and sentenced to two years and eight months after sentence reduction.

**OPEN / REQUIRES CONSOLIDATION**

- a current cumulative prison-sentence total should not be stated in this archive until all relevant judgments and concurrent / consecutive sentence treatment are reconciled from reliable sources.

## pimsiri petchnamrob

**CONFIRMED**

- sentenced on 24 february 2026 to two years and eight months in prison under section 112 in a case related to a november 2020 democracy-rally speech.
- subsequently granted bail pending appeal under conditions.

## aekachai hongkangwan

**CONFIRMED**

- human rights watch reports a september 2025 appeals-court sentence of more than 21 years for an offense related to participation in an october 2020 democracy rally.

## broader political effects

**CONFIRMED**

- reform of section 112 remained politically constrained in 2026.
- 44 opposition politicians faced proceedings over sponsorship of a bill to amend the law, with possible political disqualification.

## analytical significance

the legal response did not end when mass street protests faded.

the case demonstrates a **long-tail repression model** in which prosecution, detention and political disqualification continue years after the peak mobilization period.
