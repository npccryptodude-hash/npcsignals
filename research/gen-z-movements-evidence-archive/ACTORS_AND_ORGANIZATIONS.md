# actors and organizations

version: **0.2**

this directory records roles and documented relationships. inclusion does not imply control, shared ideology or responsibility for all actions taken within a movement.

## france

### local high-school students
role: origin / local mobilization  
status: **CONFIRMED**  
notes: school-specific grievances preceded national coordination.

### union syndicale lycéenne (usl)
role: national aggregation / representation  
status: **CONFIRMED**  
notes: called for wider national blockades after local actions had already spread.

### union étudiante
role: university bridge / mobilization infrastructure  
status: **CONFIRMED**  
notes: joined the lycée movement and coordinated joint action dates with usl.

### trade-union coalition
role: cross-sector expansion  
status: **CONFIRMED**  
notes: cfdt, cgt, fo, fsu and unsa called for the 5 november strike and demonstrations.

### yellow vest networks
role: older protest-network participation  
status: **CONFIRMED participation / NOT ESTABLISHED control**  
notes: joined or promoted wider convergence, especially around october and november national actions.

### french government / education ministry
role: state response / negotiation / policing interface  
status: **CONFIRMED**  
notes: launched consultation, announced education measures, defended intervention to preserve school access.
