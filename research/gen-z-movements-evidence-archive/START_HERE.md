## what this is about

compare youth-led movements and what follows mobilization.

## why it is examined

distinguish street mobilization, organization, elections and durable institutional change.

## what is documented

dated country files, a master timeline, actors and evidence ledgers record the archive’s comparative observations.

## what remains uncertain

long-term outcomes and causal explanations remain bounded by each country record. mobilization and electoral power are different outcomes.

## research status

ongoing research. this label describes the research work, not guilt, legal liability or the truth of every claim. source dates and evidence grades remain those in the linked records; this navigation audit adds no new findings.
