## ledger rule

a claim should be upgraded or downgraded only when new evidence is recorded with a date and source.

a repeated media claim does not become confirmed merely through repetition.

when a later source repeats an older aggregate figure without a clear new count, the archive records it as a **reported snapshot**, not automatically as a newly measured total.
