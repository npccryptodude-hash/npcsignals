# serbia 2024–2026

## short answer

serbia is the clearest current example in this archive of a student-led protest movement converting sustained mobilization into national electoral power.

## trigger

the movement followed the november 2024 collapse of the novi sad railway-station canopy, which killed 16 people and intensified public demands around accountability and alleged corruption.

## organizational development

student networks remained central during nearly two years of protest and helped sustain a broader national coalition.

## electoral outcome

**CONFIRMED**

serbia's election commission certified the october 2026 parliamentary election results.

the students' list won:

- **140 of 250 seats**;
- a parliamentary majority sufficient to form a government.

the former ruling serbian progressive party won 103 seats; minority parties hold the remaining seats.

## interpretation

**SUPPORTED**

serbia demonstrates a successful transition from:

mobilization power → organizational power → electoral power.

## not yet established

- whether electoral victory will produce durable institutional reform;
- whether the student-backed governing coalition will remain coherent;
- whether entrenched state and party structures will limit reform capacity;
- whether anti-corruption and rule-of-law commitments will be implemented.

## analytical path

grievance → sustained mobilization → durable organization → electoral vehicle → parliamentary majority → institutional test
