# master timeline

## thailand

**2020** — youth- and student-led pro-democracy protests expand demands to include constitutional reform and reform of the monarchy.

**november 2020 onward** — thai authorities resume intensive use of lèse-majesté prosecutions against democracy activists.

**2024–2026** — prominent activists remain imprisoned, prosecuted or denied bail; long-tail legal consequences continue years after the mass mobilization.

## bangladesh

**july 2024** — student protests grow from opposition to the public-sector quota system into a broader anti-government movement.

**5 august 2024** — prime minister sheikh hasina leaves the country; the army announces that she has resigned and an interim government will be formed.

**2024–2025** — accountability and institutional reform become central post-uprising issues.

**february 2026** — parliamentary election held. the established bangladesh nationalist party wins a large majority. the youth-led national citizen party wins only a small number of seats.

## serbia

**november 2024** — collapse of the novi sad railway-station canopy kills 16 people and triggers sustained protests focused on accountability and corruption.

**2024–2026** — student networks become the central organizing force in a wider national movement.

**25 october 2026** — parliamentary election.

**november 2026** — final results certified. the students' list wins 140 of 250 seats, clearing the way for a student-movement-led government.

## france

**september 2026** — high-school protests begin around teacher shortages, overcrowding, deteriorating facilities and other school conditions.

**1 october 2026** — more than 1,020 high schools are affected by strikes or protests, according to the education ministry.

**6–16 october 2026** — the movement broadens; university students, parents, teachers, unions and older protest networks increasingly appear in the wider mobilization.

**mid-october 2026** — efforts to create a wider 'convergence of struggles' become visible online and in demonstrations.

**status 7 november 2026** — france remains a live case. the long-term political outcome is not established.
