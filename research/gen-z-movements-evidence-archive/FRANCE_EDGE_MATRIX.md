## current structural reading

the network began bottom-up and later acquired centralizing interfaces.

**origin:** local schools and students  
**aggregation:** usl  
**cross-sector bridge:** union étudiante and unions  
**state interface:** education ministry / government  
**feedback loop:** police response, images of force, arrests and public debate

## not established

- a single hidden command structure;
- that political parties authored the original school grievances;
- that all violence originated from student participants;
