## preservation rule

for each external document, record:
- publisher / issuing body;
- title;
- publication date;
- access date where relevant;
- URL;
- claim IDs supported;
- whether the document is primary, monitoring, academic or journalism.

if a link later fails, the archive should preserve enough metadata to relocate the document without treating an unverified mirror as equivalent.
