# thailand — year-by-year legal table

version: **0.5**

scope: the legal and institutional aftermath of the 2020 youth-led democracy protest cycle.

this table is not a list of every prosecution. it records major milestones and aggregate indicators that help show how legal pressure developed over time.

| year | documented development | evidence status | significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | youth-led democracy protests expand nationally; monarchy reform becomes an explicit public demand in parts of the movement | CONFIRMED | establishes the protest cycle examined in this archive |
| nov 2020 | section 112 prosecutions resume against democracy activists after a hiatus | CONFIRMED | beginning of renewed lèse-majesté enforcement against movement-linked activists |
| 2021 | HRW reports rapid expansion of section 112 cases against protest participants and online expression | CONFIRMED | shows legal escalation after the protest peak |
| 2022 | emergency-decree covid restrictions are lifted in october, but protest-related cases continue | CONFIRMED | legal consequences outlast the emergency regime used during protests |
| 2023 | move forward wins the most seats in the general election but does not form the government | SUPPORTED / SOURCE LINK TO BE ADDED | relevant to electoral influence, but archive source link is still pending |
| 2024 | UN working group on arbitrary detention calls for arnon nampa's immediate release | CONFIRMED | detention and bail practices become central human-rights issues |
| 2025 | multiple monitoring sources continue to document large numbers of expression, assembly and section 112 cases | CONFIRMED / COUNTS REQUIRE CATEGORY CAUTION | demonstrates continuing legal tail |
| sep 2025 | appeals court sentences democracy activist aekachai hongkangwan to more than 21 years for an offense linked to an october 2020 rally | CONFIRMED | severe sentence tied directly to protest-cycle conduct |
| apr 2026 | HRW's UPR submission again states that at least 1,469 people linked to 2020 rallies are still being prosecuted for emergency-decree offenses | CONFIRMED AS REPORTED SNAPSHOT | the same figure appeared in earlier HRW material, so it is not treated as a fresh recount |
| 2026 | HRW World Report says arnon nampa faces 14 royal-defamation cases and reports repeated bail denial | CONFIRMED | continued prosecution of a prominent 2020 movement figure |
| 24 feb 2026 | arnon nampa and three other activists receive another section 112 conviction connected to a november 2020 rally | CONFIRMED | six-year legal tail remains active |

## archive caution

annual totals come from different reporting cutoffs and may use different inclusion rules.

the **1,469** figure is especially important: it was still cited by HRW in april 2026, but had appeared in earlier HRW reporting. the archive therefore records it as a dated source statement rather than implying a new 2026 census produced exactly the same total.
