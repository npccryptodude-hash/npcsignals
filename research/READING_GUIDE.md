# how to use this archive

[all research](INDEX.md) · [project home](../README.md)

## choose your route

- **first visit:** open a subject from the research index and read its purpose, documented findings and uncertainty.
- **school work:** follow the timeline, choose one claim, then compare its evidence and limitations. cite the dated source and the archive path, not just a headline.
- **journalism:** use the source ledger and original document or transaction identifier. distinguish a publisher’s claim from the archive’s independent checks.
- **research review:** inspect methodology, exact datasets, version history, checksums and reproducibility scripts.

## evidence strength

| label | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| confirmed | directly established within the stated source and verification scope |
| supported | concrete evidence supports the interpretation, but a decisive verification is missing |
| reported | attributed to a source; not independently established by this archive |
| disputed | sources or interpretations conflict; the conflict must remain visible |
| unresolved | the available evidence does not support a conclusive answer |

older records also use specialist terms such as derived, contradicted, not established or hold. those original classifications are preserved. this guide does not retroactively convert them into different findings.

a successful transaction proves a recorded movement within its verification scope. it does not by itself establish who controlled a wallet, whether consent existed or who ultimately benefited. timing and missing records do not prove guilt.

## research status

| status | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ongoing investigation | reconstruction of a particular event or case continues |
| ongoing research | a broader question, comparison or method remains under study |
| under verification | material validation or reconciliation remains open |
| completed | the stated scope has documented completion; unresolved external questions can still exist |
| archived | a dated research version or framework is retained as a historical reference |

none of these labels is a verdict about a person. no case has been newly marked completed by this archive audit. a frozen dataset can belong to ongoing research.

## original sources and historical records

read the retrieval time and coverage statement. a saved response may describe only a bounded period, a sample or one provider. a source that is unavailable today can still have a preserved historical capture; a successful link does not authenticate its contents.

older checkpoints can contain instructions written during research. they are historical provenance, not current instructions to the reader. current reader pages replace their role as entry points. corrections, rejected hypotheses and explicit limitations are retained.

check the [checksum exception review](../archive-maintenance/HASH_EXCEPTION_REVIEW.json) and the [source-link guide](../archive-maintenance/SOURCE_LINKS.md) before relying on a checksum or running a historical script. evidence files have not been renamed to make the interface simpler.
