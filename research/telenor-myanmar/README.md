# telenor / myanmar — research dossier

status: preliminary research / not publication-ready
updated: 2026-11-15
editorial principle: document, not judge

## scope
An educational evidence archive on Telenor Myanmar, historical traffic data disclosures, the 2022 sale, Norwegian state oversight, criminal investigations and civil litigation. Separate allegations, confirmed events, corporate responses, and unresolved questions. Do not publish identifying information about vulnerable customers.

## confirmed procedural facts
- 1 February 2021: Myanmar military coup.
- 25 March 2022: Telenor Myanmar sale completed, per Norwegian police.
- 15 September 2026: Kripos announced Telenor ASA was charged in an investigation of possible complicity in crimes against humanity in relation to repeated disclosures of historical traffic data after the coup. This is a charge, not a finding of guilt.
- On the same day PST announced a separate sanctions-law investigation relating to surveillance equipment included in the sale without ministry authorization; Telenor ASA was charged. The two agencies searched Telenor's headquarters.
- Telenor stated employees faced risks of imprisonment, torture or death if orders were refused and disputes criminal wrongdoing.
- 17 September 2026: reporting says a district court permitted a group action concerning at least 1,253 people to proceed. Permission to proceed is not a judgment on liability; subsequent appeal status needs direct court verification.

## essential distinctions
Historical traffic metadata is not the content of communications. Separate data requests, technical interception capability, equipment transfer and later use by authorities. Allegations of resulting harm require evidence of causal linkage; do not assume every data disclosure caused an arrest.
