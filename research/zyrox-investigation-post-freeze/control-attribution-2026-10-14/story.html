03 / what connects the cases?

transaction coordination is not shared ownership

glitch / direct technical result

six token sources required their own authorizations. the receiving owner signed and paid the fee. this proves one coordinated consolidation transaction.

confirmed / defined transaction

zyrox / bounded key comparison

seven signer keys and five first-returned funding sources were checked against two baseline zyrox research trees. there were no exact address-text matches in that defined sample.

bounded negative / not global absence

u300 / historical sol route

four transfers totaling 472.929423997 sol reached a destination publicly labelled binance infrastructure. three predate the zyrox creator purchase; the fourth is mixed.

transfers documented / destination label supported

6hr7 → 56heq / historical usdt route

995.831402 usdt moved in september to a historical counterparty. an onward 995.831502 usdt movement included a distinct 0.000100 usdt inflow. these are pre-launch events.

historical movements / beneficiary unresolved

the u300 and 6hr7 paths are separate historical routes. neither route proves that glitch and zyrox were run by one party, or that exchange infrastructure represents a specific customer account or token-sale proceeds.
