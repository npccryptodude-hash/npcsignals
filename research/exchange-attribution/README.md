# exchange-attributed wallet transfers

[all research](../INDEX.md) · [how to read evidence](../READING_GUIDE.md)

**research status: under verification**

## what this is about

document case-linked transfers to addresses publicly attributed to exchange infrastructure.

## why it is examined

separate a verified transfer from an exchange label, customer credit and human ownership. this is a different subject from the binance incident.

## what is documented

the preserved five-transfer evidence package records four native sol transfers and one usdt transfer to the same receiving owner, with exact amounts and receipts.

## what remains uncertain

historical custody, customer identity, internal booking and exclusive origin of commingled funds remain unresolved. a public label alone does not identify an exchange customer.

## research status

under verification. this label describes the research work, not guilt, legal liability or the truth of every claim. source dates and evidence grades remain those in the linked records; this navigation audit adds no new findings.
