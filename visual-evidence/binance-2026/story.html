01 / the essential facts

one incident. several different questions.

~$6.34breported incident-wide impact across networks — not fully reconciled by npcsignals
700m usdtselected canonical ethereum token flow; first-hop logs matched through two public rpc endpoints
1–7 novfirst week of documented aftermath: official accounts, reported freezing, replenishment and service restoration

binance is a cryptocurrency exchange. like other exchanges, it relies on technical systems and signing permissions to move assets. the reported 29 october incident involved a compromise of signing-related systems. the public blockchain shows what was moved; it does not, by itself, explain how every credential was obtained or who physically operated each address.

how to read this page: rpc matched means named transfer logs were checked against two ethereum rpc endpoints. source reported means a cited organization describes events not reproduced from raw incident infrastructure by npcsignals. official attribution means an agency has publicly assigned responsibility, not that npcsignals independently established the actor's identity. full evidence rules.

03 / conceptual mechanism

three different layers of the breach

01 / access

developer compromise

binance reports malicious developer tooling and recruitment malware used as entry points. this is the company's technical reconstruction.

02 / authorization

signing and delegation

the reported attacker gained access affecting wallet signing. eip-7702 allows delegated account execution; the authorization and later transfer are distinct on-chain events.

03 / asset transfer

movement and distribution

ethereum receipts record token transfers. subsequent hops can be traced without assuming the recipients' identities or the success of any swap.

05 / 1–7 november

the numbers that must never be confused

reported company estimate

~$6.34 billion affected

binance's preliminary total across the incident. not independently reconstructed across chains by npcsignals.

snapshot-dependent estimates

~$2.8–2.85 billion frozen / intercepted

overlapping company and third-party snapshot estimates around 3 november; not two totals to add and not equivalent to money returned.

~98% replenished

binance reported replacement of the impacted amount using its resources by 4 november; not 98% recovered from the attackers.

chainalysis described conversion to approximately $285m in eth. these cross-chain findings are not recreated from receipts in our ethereum archive.

06 / what you can check yourself

from a sentence to its underlying source

for a first-time reader

use the chronological case guide for definitions and more detail. compare the event timeline above with the linked official reports. ask whether each claim is an observation or someone else's description.

for a technical reviewer

open the transaction archive, examine the branch graph, inspect b013–b015 receipts and the evidence register. the methodology standard explains corrections and classifications.

eip-7702
a mechanism for delegated account execution; by itself it does not identify the person authorizing or exploiting it.
transfer log
an event recorded by a token contract, identifying token, sender, receiver and amount.
frozen funds
assets prevented from moving; freezing does not automatically return them to a victim.
replenished funds
assets replaced by the platform using its own resources; not necessarily attacker funds recovered.

the incident's complete cross-chain accounting, direct overlap with the fbi address set, operators of specific wallets, and final destinations remain unresolved or outside this research snapshot. this page is a static explanation, not live surveillance or a claim of legal proof.
