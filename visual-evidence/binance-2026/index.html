04 / data integrity warning

token symbols are not identities

the receiver's outgoing explorer feed includes a token named "tether usd" with symbol "usdt" at contract 0x11D0647b8Fee5662eAb6f0257404Ee57C7261cAc. this is not the canonical ethereum usdt contract 0xdAC17F958D2ee523a2206206994597C13D831ec7. symbol-labelled amounts alone cannot be counted as movements of stolen usdt.

09 / contract-state check and continuation

175m address: not blacklisted at observed heads

two independent ethereum mainnet rpc services returned false from tether's canonical usdt isBlackListed(address) for 0xaa246bf6…654a0 on 30 october 2026. this checks the current contract flag at two recent block heights. it does not establish historical status or explain why the funds remained at the address.

80m branch: full onward distribution

the 80m usdt transfer from the earlier 130m branch was also traced onward: 20 canonical-usdt events transferred the full 80m to 11 destinations. these 20 events plus the later 30m onward event matched two rpc providers (b015). the 30m is a subset of the 80m; both are already counted in prior hops.

11 / retained canonical usdt and pool receipts

30m usdt / real contract versus lookalike token

the unlabelled 30m recipient forwarded the full amount to a new address. blockscout's snapshot reports 30m canonical usdt at that next address and no outgoing canonical-usdt events from it. a later event bearing the same "usdt" ticker was issued under a different token contract and is excluded.

liquidity transaction logs / complete snapshot

the two pool-related transactions generated 64 and 68 token-transfer events (both fully paginated). neither showed a direct token transfer back to the initiating 80m sender. this does not establish where any trade output went, or that a cash-out occurred. transaction execution and downstream attribution require further review.

12 / interpretation

what this view does not prove

it does not prove how signatures were obtained, who controlled the operator address, whether any individual actor was responsible, or whether frozen assets have been recovered. exchange address labels are attribution claims and require separate corroboration.
